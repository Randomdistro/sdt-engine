# The Triton-Contraction Rule

*Phase 2026-07-23 — J. C. Harvey, Melbourne. Established against measured boundary radii (IAEA
ground states / Angeli–Marinova) crossed with the deuteron–triton construction grammar.*

## Statement

> **Adding a triton draws the nuclear boundary in.** The triton scaffold seats *tighter* than the
> deuteron filling, so as the triton count n_t climbs, the proton displacement boundary contracts —
> and it contracts **hardest where a shell closes.**

Not "charge radius" — the **boundary radius**, the proton displacement boundary. No charge substance.

## Mechanism (why, in SDT terms)

Nothing is held from within. The spation pressure holds every structure together, and matter settles
into the configuration that offers the pressure its **path of least resistance**. The interleaved
triton (1p·2n, a 3-fold interleave) is a *tighter* least-resistance seat than the deuteron (1p·1n,
2-fold), so a nucleus made more of tritons packs its boundary in. Mass defect and structure are the
same fact: the pressure's least-resistance solution, not an internal glue.

## Evidence (measured, this phase)

- **Isotope chains:** effective r0 = R / A^(1/3) falls as f_t = n_t/(n_d+n_t) rises in **88%** of
  chains (32 tested; mean correlation −0.75).
- **Isobars (fixed A, so the A^(1/3) size trend is removed):** the boundary contracts as n_t rises in
  **90%** of lines; median **~4 milli-fm per triton**, up to **~11** in the Sn/Te/Xe region. Example,
  A=124: Sn-124 (n_t=24) is 0.093 fm tighter than Xe-124 (n_t=16) at identical mass.
- **Shell modulation (3-point kink curvature, full chart so the crossings are sampled):** compaction
  is **maximal ON a shell closure** — −17 milli-fm on a neutron closure, −25 on a proton closure,
  fading to ~0 mid-shell. **Every magic number complies:** N = 20(−6) 28(−32) 50(−22) 82(−12) 126(−4).
  Openers relax to the smooth trend; mid-shell deformation can locally swell the boundary
  (e.g. Ca-44: Z=20 closed but N=24 mid-shell → swollen).

## Standing (honest bound)

Because n_t = N − Z, the *numbers* of this rule are shared with the standard isospin / symmetry-energy
term — the standard model **parametrises** the same trend without a picture. The grammar supplies the
**mechanism** (interleaved triton compaction under pressure). A clean native win — a number the
standard fit cannot claim — awaits deriving the **~11 milli-fm/triton** from the triton-interleave
geometry with zero free parameters. Until then: **native mechanism, convergent numbers.**
