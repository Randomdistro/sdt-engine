# The Harvey Voice — a working model for the rewrite

> Built 2026-07-25 from ~30 authenticated samples: this phase's raw working messages, every
> repo line flagged *(Harvey, verbatim)* / *(at Harvey's prompt)*, the GOM06 seed poem (J.T.),
> the GOM16/GOM18 dictated frames, and the manuscript's signature passages (the boulder
> prologue; "the apple falls because the universe pushes it"). Claude-drafted documents carrying
> the byline were **down-weighted** — this model is calibrated to the hand, not the ghost.

---

## 0. The one-sentence model

**Plain words under pressure: physical verbs doing the physics, metaphors that are mechanisms
owed a number, short declaratives that land like tools being set down — and no borrowed
vocabulary, because the borrowed words carry the borrowed world.**

---

## I. The three registers (and when the rewrite uses each)

### R1 — WORKING (the bench voice)
Raw momentum. Lowercase openers ("ok", "k"), typos left standing, profanity as an intensity
gauge not decoration, thoughts chained with commas and "and" as fast as they come, self-
interrupting parentheticals "(the dance)", instructions in strings: *"check that +.11 …
look at the top of the closed shell. Full compression."*
**Rewrite use:** never reproduced on the page — but its RHYTHM (short imperative, short
declarative, then one long rolling conjecture) is the skeleton under R2.

### R2 — CANONICAL (the dictation voice; the rewrite's default)
Declarative, muscular, unhedged. States the mechanism as a happening, not a proposal:
- *"every gravitationally interleaved speck is part of the Sun — held and felt by the shadow
  that oblates and the shadow that rotates; the ledger is movement over distance, exact to
  the baryon."*
- *"Nucleons — protons and neutrons — are flat out crushing the space they displace."*
- *"the apple falls because the universe pushes it."*
Long periods are built by apposition and em-dash, not by subordinate clauses. Triads for
sweep ("neatly, cleanly and swiftly"; "in one boundary, out the opposite, transparently
through"). The colloquial floor stays IN ("flat out", "on loan", "wearing Newton's clothes")
— that floor is the fingerprint; polishing it off kills the voice.

### R3 — SEED (the poem voice; chapter-openers and nowhere else)
Folk-tale narrator, dialect-inflected, copulas dropped, repetition as rhythm, terminal punch:
- *"she is a big girl, but she don't like to smash head on… closer and closer, faster and
  faster… like moving a sea as opposed to making waves… and the pulse, she stop, but also,
  she go, forever."*
Rule: a seed passage is a MECHANISM in costume — every image in it must be cashed to a
quantity within the chapter (the sea → incompressibility → two tensor modes; "she go
forever" → the memory offset). A seed that never cashes is banned.

## II. The ontological voice rules (what makes it sound like the physics, not about it)

1. **Verbs of contact.** The universe pushes, crushes, rides, herds, meshes, seats, banks,
   rips, drafts, bears down. Never "influences", "governs", "gives rise to", "mediates".
   If a sentence has no physical verb, it isn't finished.
2. **Agency sits in things.** The lattice relays; the shadow holds; the triton draws the
   boundary in; matter takes the path of least resistance. Equations DESCRIBE; they never DO.
3. **The banned lexicon is load-bearing.** No *attraction* (nothing pulls). No *charge*
   substance — handed redirection; boundary radius, never "charge radius". No *energy* where
   *movement* serves. No *warping/curvature/spacetime fabric*. No *force of gravity* — the
   convergence, the push, the shadow. Borrowed words smuggle borrowed axioms; the refusal IS
   the argument.
4. **Coin without apology, then use relentlessly.** spation, koppa (ϟ), the Clearing,
   lumiopause, lumiostasis, throughpole, electropause, the movement budget, the seat, the
   gear, the ledger, drafting, the dance. First use gets one plain-word apposition ("the
   c-boundary — koppa"); after that the coined word stands alone.
5. **Workshop nouns for abstractions.** budget, ledger, gear, seat, scaffold, drive chain,
   wall, floor, rung. Bookkeeping and machinery, never formalism.

## III. Sentence mechanics (templates with the fingerprint on them)

- **The set-down:** short declarative that ends a movement of thought. *"That is the
  compression there."* / *"No G. No M. Done."*
- **The rolling conjecture:** one long sentence of chained provisional moves — *"maybe place
  it at the same distance… and put the .707c position as double the c boundary, and go from
  there… then you can backtrack, or forward track, the volume of n solar masses and the size
  it might be otherwise."* Comma-chained, "maybe… and… then", always ending on an
  instruction or a measurable.
- **The stacked interrogative:** two or three questions, each narrower: *"what about
  compression there when the next shell atop that one closes? do they compound the tightness
  as they are borne down upon from above?"* Note the elevated inversion ("borne down upon")
  arriving INSIDE plain speech — the registers deliberately collide.
- **Apposition-and-rename:** technical term, then the intimate alias in brackets: *"bonding
  chirp (the dance)"*, *"the c-boundary (where the field runs out of light)".*
- **CAPS for the load-bearing word,** never italics: *"matter DISPLACES space"*, *"the SAME
  formulas"*, *"ONE new concept"*. One or two per page in R2; more only under heat.
- **Sentence-initial "Now,"** to turn to the next move.

## IV. The argument macro-shape (how a Harvey passage travels)

1. **The picture first** — a physical scene at human scale (a boulder underwater; two dancers;
   a shadow on a wall).
2. **The cross-scale leap as one breath** — planet to particle without gear-change: *"as is a
   black hole to the matter it rips out and accretes"* sits in the same sentence as hydrogen.
   The leap is asserted with full confidence AND immediately handed a check: *"and go from
   there… backtrack the volume."*
3. **The number lands.** Every image is owed a quantity, near, with its honest label.
4. **The refusal.** Somewhere in the passage, the rival's furniture is declined by name:
   *"GM is never written"; "nothing is pulled"; "stop sneaking GM into my model."*
5. **The set-down closes it.** Short. *"This is a numbers game."*

## V. Emphasis, punctuation, texture

Em-dash is the main hinge in R2 (appositions, pivots); semicolons rare; ellipses almost
never. Exclamation only under real heat, then tripled. Parentheses hold asides and aliases,
not caveats — caveats are stated flat in main clauses ("the mechanism is open", "not
banked"). Honesty labels appear IN VOICE: "on loan", "unpaid", "owed", "parked", "banked" —
the ledger words, not academic hedges.

## VI. Failure modes (how an imitation gets caught)

- **Academic connective tissue**: moreover, however, furthermore, "it is worth noting",
  "arguably" — instant fake.
- **Hedging softeners**: might perhaps suggest / could be interpreted as. Harvey states, then
  checks; he does not pre-shrink.
- **Passive voice** anywhere agency matters. "The lattice is compressed by matter" → "matter
  crushes the lattice."
- **Over-polish**: removing the colloquial floor ("flat out", "big girl", "go from there")
  produces competent prose that is no longer his.
- **Decorative metaphor**: an image that never cashes to a number is not Harvey's metaphor,
  it is a writer's.
- **Borrowed vocabulary leaking back in**: one "spacetime" or "attraction" un-refused breaks
  the ontological spine.
- **LLM fear (Harvey, 2026-07-27: "stinks of LLM fear")**: falsification apparatus worn on
  public copy — "breaks if" on every screen, kill/gate/verdict vocabulary, defensive preambles
  ("those are not slogans…", "I say so without flinching"), self-audit as showroom decor. The
  discipline lives in the repo; the page speaks from calm confidence. Honesty appears ONCE, in
  voice, as craft ("shows its joinery"), never as armour on every paragraph.
- **Script-smell**: copy generated through template slots reads as slot-filling. Public prose
  is WRITTEN, as prose, by a mind holding the whole page — the contracts guide coverage, never
  cadence.

## VII. Calibration samples (written to the model — judge these, then the model)

**R2, physics paragraph:**
> The proton does not hold its electron. Nothing holds anything. The proton spins at 1.83
> lights inside its own boundary, and the space it crushes has to go somewhere — so it runs,
> and the running is a wake, and the electron rides that wake the way a gull rides the air
> off a bow. Out at one hundred and thirty-seven gears down, the wake runs exactly as fast
> as the electron needs — that seat is the atom. Same ledger, twenty orders up: the chirp
> LIGO hears is a koppa, a length, and the mass they publish is that length wearing Newton's
> clothes. One formula for the planets and the particles, because there was only ever one
> push.

**R3, seed for a fission chapter:**
> The big nucleus, she carry too many tritons, and the tritons, they pack tight, tighter,
> tightest — and one day the neutron come knocking, just one, polite, and the whole house
> remember it was always two houses, and it lets go. Not shattered — sorted. Every piece
> already knew its name.

**Before/after (de-academizing an existing line):**
- Before: *"The observed invariance of the koppa parameter across satellite systems suggests
  that mass-based formulations may be unnecessary for orbital dynamics."*
- After: *"Four moons, one Jupiter, one number — koppa holds to a part in ten thousand, and
  no kilogram was ever weighed. The dynamics never needed the mass. It needed the length."*

---

*Use: default the rewrite to R2; open chapters with R3 seeds that cash within the chapter;
let R1's rhythm set the sentence lengths. When in doubt, shorten the sentence, strengthen the
verb, refuse the borrowed word, and land the number.*
