# Seat Correlations — Stress-Tested (Self-Audit)

> An earlier draft of this file presented the neutron's mass, moment, and lifetime as "three
> readings of one seated electron" and called it a unification. Under stress that framing does not
> survive. This version states what actually holds, leads with the objection I failed to raise,
> and demotes the rest to what it is: a **consistent re-description of three measured numbers, not
> a derivation** — and one with an **unresolved electron-confinement problem** that also undercuts
> NP17. **Author:** J. C. Harvey, Melbourne. *Comfort kills; this is the stress.*

---

## 1. The blow I never struck: electron confinement

The whole seat picture puts an electron at femtometre scale — at `R_p ≈ 0.84 fm` (the neutron
seat), in the `~1.9 fm` deuteron gap (NP17), or at the `0.28 fm` node. Localising an electron to
size `Δx` costs momentum-energy `~ ℏc/Δx`:

| location | `Δx` | `ℏc/Δx` |
|---|---|---|
| proton `R_p` (neutron seat) | 0.84 fm | **235 MeV** |
| deuteron gap (NP17 binding) | 1.94 fm | **102 MeV** |
| trefoil node `r_node` | 0.28 fm | **705 MeV** |

The electron's Compton wavelength is **386 fm**; the seat squeezes it `~400×` below that. The
**confinement cost is 50–300× larger than the energies the model is trying to explain** (the
0.782 MeV neutron surplus, the 2.224 MeV deuteron binding). This is precisely the 1930s
**"no electrons in the nucleus"** objection — the reason the proton-electron nuclear model was
abandoned. I built five documents on a seated internal electron and **never addressed it.**

**The two horns (pick one — both break what I built):**
- **The seat-electron MOVES** (I used `v_material = 0.919c` for the mass, `v_phase` for the
  moment) → it is a localised, propagating electron → it pays the `~235 MeV` confinement cost,
  which dwarfs the `1.3 MeV` it is meant to account for. **Fatal.**
- **The seat-electron is PINNED** (a static `W=1` topological defect — SDT's "matter = pinned
  defects," which *would* dodge the de-Broglie/confinement cost) → then it **has no velocity**, so
  the `v = 0.919c` mass derivation and the current-loop moment derivation are **void.**

I used the classical, *moving*, point-charge electron (current-loop `μ`, electrostatic `q q/r`)
**and** implicitly needed it to evade confinement. **You cannot have both, and I never said which
the SDT seat-electron is.** Until that is resolved, the seat numerology has no mechanical footing.

## 2. The seat "correlation" is a re-description, not a derivation

Stripped of the flattering framing, here is what each "result" actually was:

- **`Q_β = m_n − m_p − m_e = 0.782 MeV`** — true, but it is the β-decay Q-value, which **standard
  physics reproduces identically.** It is *not* evidence for the composite; any model has it. I
  presented model-neutral arithmetic as a composite-model success.
- **`v_material = 0.919c`** — *back-solved* from `γ_e m_e = m_n − m_p`, on the unforced assumption
  that the *entire* mass surplus is the electron's kinetic energy (it could be field/configuration
  energy). Not a prediction.
- **The moment's "exact sign"** — I *posited* counter-rotation **because** `μ_n < 0`. The sign is
  an **input, not an output.** Circular.
- **`r_eff ~ R_p`** — *solved from* the moment; "of order `R_p`" is a one-bit sanity check, not a
  result.

So the "three numbers hang on one electron" was three measured numbers fed *into* a model that can
accommodate them, dressed as a convergence. **It predicts none of them independently.**

## 3. The α number was inflated by a circular road

I featured "four independent roads to `1/α ≈ 137`, the sharpest at 0.02%." **The sharpest road was
circular:** `r_e/R_p = (α/4)(m_p/m_e)`, so using measured `r_e/R_p` to get `v_p = 1.830` *feeds in
α*, and "predicting" 137.07 is a tautology. The **genuinely independent** estimate — the mass
defect `→ v_phase = 1.837c` — gives **`1/α ≈ 136.0`, which is ~0.7% off 137.036, not 0.02%.** And
of the "four roads," the trefoil engine is *tuned* to 1.836 and the three-speed average is the
*same source*. Honest count: **one independent road, landing ~0.7% from α.** The α-from-seat
closure is real only at the percent level, and weaker than I let on.

## 4. The `√(W+1)` was a dressed-up factor of 2

`v_phase/v_material = 1.837/0.919 = 1.999`. I called it `√(W+1) = √4` because `W=3` makes it land.
That is imposing SDT vocabulary on a **bare factor of 2** relating two soft (back-solved / tuned)
numbers to 0.4%. A 1-in-~250 agreement between two model-dependent quantities is **not** a
correlation; calling it `√(W+1)` manufactured significance.

## 5. What actually survives the stress

Very little, predictively:
- The **arithmetic** `m_n − m_p − m_e = 0.782 MeV` (but model-neutral).
- The composite `n = p⁺ + e⁻` as an **ontological posit** — *not disproven*, but *unconfirmed* by
  these numbers, and now carrying the **confinement debt of §1** as its central liability.
- That is it. The velocities, the moment, the `√(W+1)`, the α-closure, and the "unification" do
  **not** survive as derivations.

## 6. What it would take to make any of it real

1. **Resolve §1 first** — define the SDT seat-electron precisely (pinned topological defect vs
   moving wake) and show it **either** evades the `~235 MeV` confinement cost **or** that the cost
   is paid by something (the `1.293 MeV` is then a *small residual* on a large balanced ledger).
   Until this exists, NP17's deuteron "PASS" and the neutron seat are **both** on sand.
2. If pinned: **redo the mass and moment without a velocity** — find what a static `W=1` defect's
   contribution to mass and `μ` actually is. The `0.919c` and the current loop are then wrong tools.
3. **Derive `G_F`** (the seat-release rate) for the lifetime — still the deepest open piece.
4. Only after 1–3 is the `√(W+1)` worth testing as anything but a coincidence.

**Net (stress verdict):** the seat-correlation, as I built it, is a re-parameterisation of three
known numbers with no independent prediction, a percent-level (not ppm) α-link via one
non-circular road, and an **unaddressed confinement objection that also threatens NP17.** The
honest status is *open with a serious liability*, not *closed*. The comfort version is deleted.
