In ordinary English

Space is not empty. It is a packing of contacting cells — spations — that hand movement to their neighbours, one cell per tick. The tick is so short that light is just the handoff rate: c.

Matter is not a guest in that packing. It is a lasting dent — a knot of circulating movement that excludes a volume of cells. Force is what happens when one dent blocks the inflow that would otherwise reach another. Nothing pulls. The universe pushes, and a closed path of least resistance through that push is an orbit.

One speed. No first moment. No last one either.
