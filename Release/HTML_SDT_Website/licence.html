What is not claimed

  • No claim is made about what existed before the Clearing, or whether the lattice is finite.
  • Results carry explicit certification labels — DERIVED, COMPUTED, IDENTITY, CALIBRATED, OBSERVED, PENDING — and a label is part of the claim. An item marked PENDING is an open problem, not a quiet success.
  • Where a prediction shares its form with standard physics, the position taken is that the experiment cannot discriminate — not that the standard result was derived here.
