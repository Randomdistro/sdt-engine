Abstract. Galactic rotation is treated with zero halo parameters. The acceleration floor is derived, a₀ = cH₀/2π ≈ 1.04 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s²; a deliberately wrong floor degrades the observed radial-acceleration collapse sixfold, establishing load-bearing status. Mass at galactic scale is exhibited as collective spatial displacement — a pressure gradient of the substrate — of which the koppa point-source law is the dilute limit; the root relation zd = h(𝔇) carries no fitted coefficient in the stated expression. On the rotation-curve database the audited record is mixed and reported in full: the canonical three-dimensional eclipse construction FAILS real photometry (RMS 66%; an earlier success traced to a circular mock and was voided); the twin-regime construction reaches 23.8% but imports its crossover shape; the drafting-crossover pilot re-earned 8.8% on direct rerun with a 6× control margin, its shape still borrowed and labelled so. A mass-free koppa-density regression spans 693 systems at 5.9%. The streak analysis removes a +35% halo-inference bias to +0.2%. Spiral structure follows from winding arithmetic: ~62 turns per 10 Gyr forces standing pressure waves with a spoke clock of 162 Myr.

1. The derived floor

a₀ = c·H₀/(2π) = 1.04 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s²  [C-flagged: inherits H₀'s structure caveat](1)

The floor is the residual convergence-depth deficit that interstellar space cannot shed — the relay ceiling's imprint (galactic depth floor zgal ≈ 3.5 × 10⁻⁷; absolute ceiling c∞ = c/(1−zgal) ≈ c + 105 m/s). Its load-bearing status is tested, not asserted: substituting a deliberately wrong floor degrades the radial-acceleration collapse by a factor 6. The floor's numerical proximity to the phenomenological value used by the modified-inertia rival is a convergence of independent origins, disclosed; the rival's interpolation shape is not derived here, and every use of a borrowed shape below is labelled.

2. Mass as collective displacement

zd = h(𝔇) — no fitted coefficient in the stated expression; koppa point-source = dilute limit(2)

At galactic surface densities the displacement fields of ~10¹¹ sources cease to superpose as isolated shadows: the medium carries one collective pressure gradient. The koppa law of Paper 06 is recovered exactly in the dilute limit; the collective regime is governed by the root relation (2) with no fitted constant (GD06). Eclipse saturation is the geometric statement: when integrated disk occlusion reaches its critical fraction, the shadow stops deepening with radius and v(r) = c√(ϟ/r) flattens onto the floor (1).

4. The winding arithmetic and spiral structure

Nturns ≈ 62 per 10 Gyr  ⟹  material arms impossible; spoke clock 162 Myr(3)

Differential rotation would wind material arms ~62 times in 10 Gyr; observed grand-design structure therefore cannot be material. In the collective-displacement medium the arms are standing pressure waves — interference spokes of the disk's own displacement field — with a natural clock of 162 Myr per spoke cycle. No density-wave amplitude is fitted; the clock follows from the same kinematics as (2).
