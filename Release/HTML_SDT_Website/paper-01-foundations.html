Abstract. Spatial Displacement Theory begins with a gap-free contact-relay substrate whose pitch ℓP is Axiom R1. With the observed boundary scale RCMB, the Law-I shell accounting defines N = RCMB/ℓP, Φ = Nε, Pconv = Φ/ℓP³, and S = 4πN². The factor S is a causal-depth count and therefore explains where the familiar order-10¹²³ comparison enters this accounting; it is not an independent measurement of vacuum energy. FLM06 does not derive ℓP, and every circular recovery remains classified as an identity. FLM12 supplies a candidate relay-imbalance account of inertia and recovers Newtonian and rest-energy forms at the algebraic level, but its direct quantitative D1/D2 gates remain open. This paper therefore separates the derived shell relations from the unclosed microscopic mass mechanism.

1. Axiomatic setting

The substrate is postulated as a countable set of spations in permanent mutual contact (gap-free), each individually incompressible, the ensemble collectively deformable, close-packed icosa-dodecahedrally at one pitch (Axiom R1). The single admissible transport operation is nearest-neighbour relay of movement at one pitch per tick:

c = ℓP/tP,  ℓP = 1.616255 × 10⁻³⁵ m(1)

The dependency ledger assigns roles rather than treating this list as eight equivalent primitives: ℓP is the lattice pitch; c, kB, and e are unit bridges; {ℏ, me, mp} form one action/mass seat; α is the hydrogen rung; TCMB is the present boundary clock; and directly measured observables enter only in their measured units. Every numerical claim must expose which of these roles it inherits.

2. The convergence burden

Partition the substrate between an arbitrary point and the boundary of the Clearing at RCMB = 9.527 × 10²⁶ m (an observed cosmological scale; certification class X — it conditions the Law-I chain and does not pass the delete-test) into N nested Planck-thick shells:

N = RCMB/ℓP = 5.894 × 10⁶¹(2)

The Shell Cancellation Identity (Theorem T1) states that the inverse-square dilution of a shell's relayed content is cancelled exactly by the quadratic growth of its cell count, so every shell delivers the same elementary content ε = uCMB·ℓP³ = 1.761 × 10⁻¹¹⁸ J. Theorem T2 then fixes the local burden and its pressure:

Φ = Nε = 1.038 × 10⁻⁵⁶ J(3)
Pconv = Φ/ℓP³ = N·uCMB = 2.459 × 10⁴⁸ Pa(4)

The burden is isotropic; the resultant at every unoccluded point vanishes identically. Force appears only under partial occlusion (Paper 03; Theorems T3–T4).

3. The counting resolution of the vacuum discrepancy

The boundary source count is

S = 4πN² = 4.366 × 10¹²⁴(5)

The order-10¹²³ mismatch between a naïve boundary-cell count and an observed cosmic density is, in this accounting, associated with the dimensionless factor 4πN². This is an internal counting correspondence conditioned on RCMB and ℓP. It does not constitute a new independent measurement, nor does it remove the need to test whether the shell construction describes the observed boundary.

4. Absolute-length closure (negative result)

FLM06 attempted to derive ℓP from Clearing geometry using the derivation basis {RCMB, c, kB, TCMB, zrec}. The attempt fails: every clean dimensionless ratio bottoms out near 10³ (zrec), ten orders short per factor of the required 10⁶¹. The stronger claim — that exactly one action/mass/length anchor is unavoidable in any closure of this type — is stated here as OPEN: the failure above is evidence toward it, not a proof of it. The koppa-form ℓP = √(ϟbaryon·ƛp) re-expresses the pitch through an SDT-native length but is an identity, not an elimination: ϟbaryon ≡ ℓP²·c·mp/ℏ, so the root reproduces its own input. ℓP therefore stands as the lattice pitch of Axiom R1, with the derivation attempt logged as a negative result.

5. Packing frustration and the shaped interstices

Twelve spations about one cannot close: the icosahedral 12-around-1 edge gap is 0.05146, and the canonical lattice frustration gap is exactly twice it, g = 0.1029 ≈ 0.103, to four figures. Because the substrate is everywhere in relay motion, this slack does not remain distributed: it gathers into shaped interstices (tetrahedral and octahedral pockets). These pockets recur throughout the framework — as electron seats in atomic structure and as the tetrahedral α-core geometry of nuclear construction (Paper 05). The same gap g = 0.103 re-enters as the per-generation leak of the displacement cascade (Paper 10), where it yields the first zero-parameter intermittency number.

6. Inertia as relay-Doppler imbalance (FLM12)

FLM12 proposes that translation de-tunes fore/aft relay arrival rates around a displacement structure. Angular averaging then supplies the formal coefficient m = ΦVdisp/(3ℓP³c²), with Pcf = Pconv/3 as an identity. The resulting expressions correspond to uniform force-free motion, F ∝ dv/dt, E₀ = mc², and equal inertial and gravitational response when both depend on Vdisp. Those correspondences establish algebraic compatibility, not completed microscopic dynamics. The direct D1/D2 perturbation calculations required to derive the response coefficient from relay evolution are outstanding.
