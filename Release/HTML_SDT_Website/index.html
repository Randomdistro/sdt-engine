The Medium

Chapter Four

ATOMICUS

Every stable nucleus with Z ≥ 2 decomposes into exactly one alpha core plus some number of deuterons and tritons. The triton is not merely a structural component — it is the source of nuclear magnetism. And the grammar admits two distinct lineages depending on whether the core is complete.

Standard Grammar — α core (He-4)
1α + nd + nt

EC Grammar — He-3 core (incomplete tetrahedron)
1 He-3 + nd + nt

The standard grammar builds on a complete He-4 alpha — a closed tetrahedral core. Electron capture isotopes use a He-3 core instead: an incomplete tetrahedron missing one neutron. This structural deficit is the geometric reason EC occurs — the nucleus seeks completion by capturing an orbital electron to convert a proton into a neutron, closing the tetrahedron.

The triton as magnetic source

In SDT, magnetism at the nuclear level is not a separate phenomenon — it is a geometric consequence of triton content. Each triton (one proton + two neutrons) carries an unpaired spin that the deuteron's symmetric proton-neutron pair does not. The triton count directly determines the nuclear magnetic moment. Pure deuteron scaffolds (nt = 0) are magnetically silent; every triton added introduces a new source of magnetic interaction. This is why the most magnetically active elements — iron, cobalt, nickel — sit at the triton fraction where magnetic coupling is geometrically optimised.

Natural Electron Capture

When a nucleus is built on a He-3 core — an incomplete tetrahedron — it is structurally predisposed to capture an inner-shell electron. The capture converts p → n, completing the tetrahedral alpha. The EC isotope transitions from the He-3 lineage to the α-core lineage. This is not random decay — it is geometric self-repair.

Spallation-Induced Capture

High-energy collisions can break structural bonds within the nucleus, knocking a deuteron or triton loose from the scaffold. If the damage exposes or creates an incomplete core, the resulting fragment becomes eligible for electron capture — spallation opens a recombination pathway between construction lineages that thermal processes alone would not access.

Chapter Eight

Glossary

Spation

Individual element of the spatial lattice. Individually incompressible, collectively deformable. Structured at Planck-length scale (1.616 × 10⁻³⁵ m).

Koppa (Ϟ)

ϟ = v²R/c² — one length, in metres, that carries a body's entire gravitational field. Its gear is k = c/v. Same formula for the proton and the Sun. Replaces GM/c² — no G, no kilograms, ever.

The Clearing

The epoch when every spation released its stored energy simultaneously. Replaces the Big Bang. SDT makes no claim about what existed before.

Occlusion

Matter blocking a fraction of convergent pressure from one direction, creating a net force toward the occluding body. The mechanism behind gravity, Coulomb force, and nuclear binding.

Convergence

Inward-propagating pressure from all Planck shells to every point in space. Total burden: Φ = Nε ≈ 1.04 × 10⁻⁵⁶ J.

Displacement

The volume of lattice a particle excludes. Determines both inertial mass (reorganisation cost) and gravitational mass (occlusion cross-section).

Movement Budget

v²circ + v²trans = c². Every particle's internal circulation plus translational velocity sums to the speed of light squared.

Wake Tensor

Spherical harmonic decomposition of a vortex's pressure disturbance. ℓ=1 gives Coulomb, ℓ=2 gives magnetic dipole, ℓ≥3 gives nuclear form factors.

Winding Number (W)

Topological classification of vortex structures. W=0: neutrino. W=1: electron. W=3: proton. W=2 and W≥4 are topologically unstable.

Eclipse Saturation

When a galactic disk's total occlusion reaches a critical fraction, the rotation curve flattens — producing the effect attributed to dark matter halos.

Golden Boundary

Z = 79 (Gold). The first element where triton count exceeds deuteron count (39t vs 38d). The onset of topological instability in nuclear construction grammar.

Transfer Function (f)

Ratio Peff / Pconv = 2.125 × 10⁻¹⁷. Maps convergence pressure to effective interaction pressure at atomic scale.

Traction Fraction (τ)

1/3 — the fraction of relay energy propagating in the forward direction in 3D isotropic space.

Nuclear Construction Grammar

The decomposition of every nucleus Z ≥ 2 into 1 alpha core + nd deuterons + nt tritons. Verified for all 287 naturally occurring isotopes.

Triton

One proton + two neutrons. The source of nuclear magnetism in SDT — each triton carries an unpaired spin that the symmetric deuteron does not. Triton count directly determines the nuclear magnetic moment. Pure deuteron scaffolds (nt = 0) are magnetically silent.

Electron Capture (EC)

When a nucleus built on a He-3 core (incomplete tetrahedron) captures an inner-shell electron, converting p → n and completing the alpha tetrahedron. Not random decay — geometric self-repair. EC isotopes use the alternate grammar: 1 He-3 + nd + nt.

Spallation

High-energy collision that breaks structural bonds within a nucleus, knocking deuterons or tritons loose from the scaffold. If spallation exposes an incomplete core, it opens an electron capture pathway — a recombination route between construction lineages.
