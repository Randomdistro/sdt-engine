       The HGA Pattern Calibration Report and the USO Characterization Report
       are needed for data analysis of 1-way Radio Science experiments. Since
       the SCE1 is a 2-way experiment, these reports are not included in the
       SCE1 archives.

       The documents CK_MM_SIS_000614.TXT and SPK_MM_SIS_000525.TXT are the
       latest versions that were available from NAIF.

       The second paragraph of section 2.5 of the document CASRSSIS.TXT
       should be updated as follows: Six copies of each DVD-R volume will
       be created. Two copies will be delivered to the PDS Atmospheres
       Node, one copy will be delivered to the PDS Engineering Node and
       three copies will be retained by RSSG at JPL.

       Figures 3-1.1 and 3-1.2 of the document CASRSSIS.TXT should be
       updated to include PROJREF.CAT under CATALOG.
