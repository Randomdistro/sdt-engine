     The RSR Operations Log is completed by a member of the Radio
Science Systems Group (RSSG) for each DSN pass and it contains
information such as Beginning of Track (BOT), End of Track (EOT),
One-Way Light Time (OWLT), Round-Trip Light Time (RTLT), Start
Record time, End Record Time and other information that is
related to the configuration and the operation of the RSR (e.g.,
SFRO for the RSR sub-channel frequency offset). This Log is
formatted as follows:
