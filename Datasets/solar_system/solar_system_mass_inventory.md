# Solar System Mass Inventory

## Central Star
- **Sun**: 1.989 × 10³⁰ kg (99.86% of total system mass)

## Planets
- **Mercury**: 3.301 × 10²³ kg
- **Venus**: 4.867 × 10²⁴ kg  
- **Earth**: 5.972 × 10²⁴ kg
- **Mars**: 6.417 × 10²³ kg
- **Jupiter**: 1.898 × 10²⁷ kg (largest planet, ~0.1% of system mass)
- **Saturn**: 5.683 × 10²⁶ kg
- **Uranus**: 8.681 × 10²⁵ kg
- **Neptune**: 1.024 × 10²⁶ kg

## Major Moons
### Earth System
- **Moon**: 7.342 × 10²² kg

### Mars System  
- **Phobos**: 1.07 × 10¹⁶ kg
- **Deimos**: 1.48 × 10¹⁵ kg

### Jupiter System (95 known moons)
- **Io**: 8.932 × 10²² kg
- **Europa**: 4.800 × 10²² kg  
- **Ganymede**: 1.482 × 10²³ kg (largest moon in solar system)
- **Callisto**: 1.076 × 10²³ kg

### Saturn System (146 known moons)
- **Titan**: 1.345 × 10²³ kg (2nd largest moon, thick atmosphere)
- **Rhea**: 2.31 × 10²¹ kg
- **Iapetus**: 1.81 × 10²¹ kg
- **Dione**: 1.10 × 10²¹ kg
- **Tethys**: 6.17 × 10²⁰ kg
- **Enceladus**: 1.08 × 10²⁰ kg
- **Mimas**: 3.75 × 10¹⁹ kg

### Uranus System (27 known moons)
- **Titania**: 3.53 × 10²¹ kg
- **Oberon**: 3.01 × 10²¹ kg
- **Umbriel**: 1.17 × 10²¹ kg
- **Ariel**: 1.35 × 10²¹ kg
- **Miranda**: 6.59 × 10¹⁹ kg

### Neptune System (16 known moons)
- **Triton**: 2.14 × 10²² kg (retrograde, likely captured Kuiper Belt object)

### Pluto System
- **Pluto**: 1.303 × 10²² kg (dwarf planet)
- **Charon**: 1.586 × 10²¹ kg

## Asteroid Belt (2.1 - 3.3 AU)
- **Total Belt Mass**: ~2.39 × 10²¹ kg (0.04% of Moon's mass)
- **Ceres**: 9.39 × 10²⁰ kg (dwarf planet, ~38% of belt mass)
- **Vesta**: 2.59 × 10²⁰ kg (~10% of belt mass)
- **Pallas**: 2.04 × 10²⁰ kg (~8% of belt mass)  
- **Hygiea**: 8.7 × 10¹⁹ kg (~3% of belt mass)
- **Remaining asteroids**: ~1.1 × 10²¹ kg (>1 million objects >1 km)

## Trans-Neptunian Region

### Kuiper Belt (30 - 50 AU)
- **Total estimated mass**: ~0.01 - 0.1 Earth masses (6 × 10²² - 6 × 10²³ kg)
- **Eris**: 1.66 × 10²² kg (dwarf planet, most massive known TNO)
- **Makemake**: 3.1 × 10²¹ kg (dwarf planet)
- **Haumea**: 4.0 × 10²¹ kg (dwarf planet, elongated)
- **Quaoar**: 1.4 × 10²¹ kg
- **Sedna**: ~1 × 10²¹ kg (extreme orbit)

### Scattered Disk (30 - 100+ AU)
- **Estimated mass**: Similar to Kuiper Belt classical region
- Highly eccentric, inclined orbits

### Oort Cloud (2,000 - 100,000 AU)
- **Estimated mass**: 0.4 - 40 Earth masses (2.4 × 10²⁴ - 2.4 × 10²⁶ kg)
- **Composition**: ~10¹² cometary nuclei
- **Structure**: Inner Oort cloud (2,000-20,000 AU), Outer Oort cloud (20,000-100,000 AU)

## Ring Systems
- **Saturn's rings**: ~1.5 × 10¹⁹ kg (mostly water ice)
- **Jupiter's rings**: ~10¹³ kg (dust particles)
- **Uranus rings**: ~10¹⁶ kg (dark particles)
- **Neptune rings**: ~10¹⁵ kg (dust and debris)

## Interplanetary Medium
- **Zodiacal dust**: ~10¹⁶ kg (microscopic particles)
- **Solar wind particles**: ~10¹² kg (charged particles from Sun)
- **Cosmic dust influx**: ~40,000 tons/year incoming

## Trojans and Co-orbital Objects
- **Jupiter Trojans**: ~10²⁰ kg total (two swarms at L4/L5 points)
- **Other trojan populations**: Mars, Neptune trojans (much smaller)

## Small Bodies
- **Short-period comets**: ~10¹⁸ kg total active population
- **Long-period comets**: Include Oort cloud estimates above
- **Meteoroids**: ~10¹⁵ kg (mm to meter-sized debris)
- **Near-Earth objects**: ~10¹⁸ kg (asteroids crossing Earth's orbit)

## Summary by Region
1. **Inner Solar System** (< 2 AU): Sun + 4 terrestrial planets + Moon ≈ 99.95% total mass
2. **Main Belt** (2-4 AU): ~0.0001% total mass
3. **Outer Planets** (5-30 AU): ~0.13% total mass  
4. **Trans-Neptunian** (30-100 AU): ~0.0001-0.001% total mass
5. **Oort Cloud** (2,000-100,000 AU): ~0.0002-0.02% total mass

**Total estimated system mass**: ~2.0 × 10³⁰ kg (dominated by the Sun)
