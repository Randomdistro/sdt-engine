These are the ephemeris-literature values the rewritten GOM42 prompt calls for (the great Jupiter–Saturn inequality). Limits: the Standish terms are the coefficients of a least-squares fit to a numerical ephemeris over 3000 BC–3000 AD, so the period 938.68 yr is the frequency of that fit's best term in the mean anomaly, not a quoted analytic period of the resonance; this is stated where the comparison is made.
