SPACE · MATTER · MOVEMENT · THE PRESENT

Four things exist. Everything else — every force, every constant, every line in every spectrum — is these four, arranged.

READ ON ↓

The first irreducible

Space

Space is not an emptiness that things sit in. It is a substance — grains of one size, packed shoulder to shoulder, a superfluid that reconfigures at the speed of light. It can be crowded. It can be thinned. It can carry a wake.

Pack twelve grains around one and the fit never quite closes; and because everything here moves, that slack does not lie where a frozen picture would leave it. It gathers — into shaped pockets, tetrahedral and octahedral and larger still. Remember the pockets. Atoms keep their electrons in them, and the alpha core is a tetrahedron for a reason.

The second irreducible

Matter

A particle is a knot — circulation wound on itself and locked by its own winding, crushing the space it stands in. Never a cloud, never a point: a made thing, with a boundary you can measure and a surface that runs faster than light ever travels.

And here is the one new idea this theory brings, the sentence everything else on this site unpacks: matter displaces space. The displaced grains have to go somewhere. Where they go — the crowding ahead, the wake behind, the shadow cast — is every force you have ever felt.

The third irreducible

Movement

Movement is the one currency. It is relayed grain to grain — that relay is light. It is wound into knots — that winding is matter. It arrives from the boundary of everything, every tick — that arrival is the push this cosmos runs on.

Every particle spends its whole allowance at c, always: spin and travel from one purse, v_circ² + v² = c². Stand still and it all turns inward. Move, and the travel is paid out of the spin. The slowed clocks, the shortened rods, E = mc², the speed nothing passes — all of it is this one budget, spending. Nothing dilates but the bookkeeping. Nothing in this universe ever stops. It only changes what it spends its movement on.

The fourth irreducible

The Present

It is always now. It always was. The present is not a moment sliding along a line — it is the plane of existence itself, with everything inside it moving, always moving. What you call time is the tally that movement keeps: the past is the tally kept, the future the tally expected, and neither is a place.

Nothing here exists by having existed. Every knot pays its keep this tick; every wake is arriving now; the whole history of the cosmos reaches you as present pressure. Only the plane is — and the plane does not elapse.

From the boundary of the Clearing, the release is still arriving — from every direction, every tick. Nothing in this physics pulls. The apple falls because the universe pushes it.

ONE ACTION · ONE PRESSURE · ONE LAW, READ AT EVERY REACH

One push, read three ways

Stand two knots in the arriving flood and each shades the other: the push between them thins, the push behind them does not, and the flood presses them together. Write that once — a pressure, two cross-sections, a distance — and you have written gravity, electricity, and the nuclear grip in a single line. Their famous difference in strength is a difference in reach, not in kind: the same weather, shadowed at three scales.

From there the theory walks upward without changing its stride. The same gear that seats an electron in hydrogen seats a moon around Jupiter — the same formulas for the planets as for the particles — one invariant, v²r = c²ϟ, holding from the proton to the black hole at the centre of this galaxy, twenty powers of ten on one line, with no gravitational constant and no weighed mass anywhere in it.

Fluids roll into the same tori the particles are wound from. Heat is the same movement, disordered — and what the heat-count counts is freedom. Matter climbs into structure precisely when binding opens more routes than it closes, and that ledger dates the universe's first assembly step to the very window where the measurements put it.

Beautiful things that work properly. Every claim on this site carries its number; every number is computed, not fitted; and the few pieces still being built are shown as they stand. A theory is a made thing — this one shows its joinery.
