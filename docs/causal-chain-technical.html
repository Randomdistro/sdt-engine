0. Method: what a dependency spine asserts

A causal chain in this sense is not a narrative ordering but a partial order of derivational dependency. Link Ln depends on Lm when the relation at Ln consumes a quantity whose meaning is fixed at Lm. The discipline this imposes is severe and is the point: a result may not be cited at a link earlier than the link that defines its terms, and a circular route — one whose output is algebraically its own input — must be labelled IDENTITY and never DERIVED.

Three failure modes are policed explicitly throughout. Circularity: a relation returning its own input (e.g. e = √(αℏc/ke), which is a restatement of α's definition) is labelled as such. Shared form: where an SDT relation is algebraically identical to a rival framework's at the tested order, the correspondence is disclosed and the relation is recorded as unable to discriminate. Count degeneracy: where a regularity is a pure function of quantities already fixed upstream, it is bookkeeping, not evidence (NP24-A).
