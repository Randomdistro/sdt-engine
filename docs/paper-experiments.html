Abstract. The framework carries 97 numbered experiment specifications (E13–E109) across the nuclear, cosmological, orbital, and electromagnetic sectors. Each specification states three things: the SDT prediction, the rival prediction, and the observable that discriminates. This section paper formalises the specification format, the difficulty ladder (already testable · moderate · frontier · extreme), and the programme's operating rules: predictions are pre-registered before instrumentation; instruments are validated on known answers before use; comparisons are whole-range and single-pass; verdicts — including failures — are retained on display. Worked entries are given for the convergent anchors (perihelion closure, limb deflection), the divergent discriminants (mass-independent orbital drift, substrate acoustics between shielded plates, γ-ray dispersion, the neutrino moment, post-merger echoes), and the registered rejection criteria the framework exposes itself to (monopole detection, CMB spectral distortion above 50 ppm, the solar-limb fold scan).

1. The specification format

E## := ⟨ SDT prediction · rival prediction · observable discriminant · difficulty · status ⟩(1)

A specification is admissible only if the discriminant is stated as a measurement that can return either verdict. Entries whose discriminant turns out to be shared form — both frameworks predicting the same number for reasons of their own — are re-labelled convergent anchors and removed from the discriminating set (the fragment-energy fine-tune of the beam-peel campaign was retired exactly this way). The programme's verdicts are retained: a failed construction remains on display with its verdict attached, because the record is the product.

4. Registered rejection criteria

monopole detection ⟹ B = ∇×w identification ruled out (E58) · CMB distortion > 50 ppm ⟹ release mechanism ruled out · monotone λ-scan at the solar limb ⟹ one-surface reading ruled out (OP07)(2)

The framework names, in advance, the observations that would rule its mechanisms out. The solar-fold specification carries date priority in the programme record: a false-colour wavelength scan of the limb must show the disk grow, partially vanish in a caustic fold at the 4400–5000 K temperature-minimum band, and reappear — the standard many-layer reading predicts no fold, and the protocol runs on existing narrowband imagery. Internal gates bind with the same force: the W+1 rule dies if Weff departs from the integer 3 beyond 3σ, and the closure-schedule completion law (Section Paper IV) is pre-registered as a single-pass computation of the whole docking-lock curve — if it needs re-tuning at any rung, that is the finding.

5. Operating rules of the programme

(i) Pre-registration before instrumentation. (ii) Instruments validated on known answers before use. (iii) Whole-range, single-pass comparisons — no per-item re-reading. (iv) Convergent results claimed as convergent, shared-form results as shared form, never as discrimination. (v) Verdicts retained: the rotation-curve failure, the cubic-distance exclusion, and the withdrawn frustration selector all remain on display. (vi) Counts-indexed narration is inadmissible as evidence (the NP24-A rule): a regularity that merely re-reads the ledger cannot pass as a prediction.

Dimensional and limiting-case audit

Every observable and tolerance carries a unit; normalized residuals and test statistics are dimensionless. Zero-source, infinite-separation, symmetry and boundary limits are stated wherever defined; a relation that fails unit closure is excluded before numerical comparison.

Methodological declaration. Rival frameworks appear in this programme exactly where they belong: as the named alternative in each discriminant row. No rival machinery is used to generate any SDT prediction. The programme's authority rests on its retained verdicts — the failures displayed beside the passes — and on the pre-registration discipline that prevents a prediction from being written after its answer.

Falsification

A specification is incomplete unless its rejection class is physically reachable and its comparator uses the same observable.

Limitations and open problems

A registered experiment is not evidence until executed by a validated instrument on identified data.
