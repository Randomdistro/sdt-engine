# E111 — Actinide flay vs liquid-drop split

**Author:** J. C. Harvey, Melbourne. **Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** ⮑ GRADUATED → `Investigations/05_Nuclear_Physics/NP35_Flay_Not_Split/`

## SDT prediction

A driven actinide is **flayed**. Unfrozen outer shells unwrap. The heavy remnant is the outermost **sealed triton shell** (T12, N=82) while D42 is incomplete so T14 is not sealed. Pre-neutron A_H ≈ 133–135, nearly independent of parent A. Stop-at-N=126 (Pb-like core) is the rival complete-closure unwrap and is excluded.

## Standard picture

Bohr–Wheeler liquid drop plus shell corrections: a necked split of a deformable charged drop; asymmetry from shell corrections at scission (N=82 / Z=50 inserted).

## Observable difference

Independent mass-yield heavy peak near A≈134–140 for U/Pu/Th/Np/Am, **not** near A≈205–210. Heavy peak stationary while light peak slides with parent A.

## Feasibility

Archival IAEA independent FY (eight systems already cached). Superheavy N≥168 (predicted switch to N_H=126) needs ENDF SF/FY not on relnsd.

## Impact

If F3 holds and F1 stays excluded, fission products are a jacket vs a masonry core — in principle selectable by flay depth — not a statistical drop split.
