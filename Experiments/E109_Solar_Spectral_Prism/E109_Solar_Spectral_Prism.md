## 1. The claim

The standard solar model reads the wavelength-dependent appearance of the Sun as a stack of
emission layers: radio from the corona, mm from the chromosphere, optical from the
photosphere, with a "temperature-minimum layer" sampled twice (far-IR and UV). SDT reads it
as **one emitter wrapped in one dispersive displacement shell**: a spherical prism. The
"emission depths" are separation surfaces — a mirage. Each wavelength is a movement
(λ = 2L·c/v); the shell's displacement gradient, loaded with the medium's seats, re-gears
each span differently along the entire traverse. Banding is what a displacement gradient
does to a mixed bundle of gears.

## 3. THE PRE-REGISTERED NUMBER — the far-IR minimum position

The entire observational record for the far-IR brightness-temperature minimum is: one
±260 K band measurement longward of the minimum (1969, aircraft), balloon spectra (1975),
and three broad filters at 50/80/200 μm (Degiacomi et al., 1981 flight). **The minimum's
wavelength has never been resolved.** Three readings now disagree about it:

| reading | predicted position | mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **SDT-A: between-rung null** | **153.3 μm** = √(H14α × H15α) | two adjacent hydrogen-ladder seat responses arriving superposed, cancelling between the rungs ("twice in the centre") |
| **SDT-B: H⁻ decade** | **164.4 μm** = λ(E(H⁻)/100) | the caustic seat's gear structure |
| **Rival (SM layers)** | ~150 μm, **no exact number** — wherever H⁻ free-free τ=1 crosses the temp-min layer; model-dependent | stratified atmosphere |

SDT-A is favoured (its UV twin retrodicts Samain to 0.03%). A resolved measurement of the
far-IR minimum position decides three ways. Instrument class: stratospheric FTS/balloon or
airborne photometry, 120–200 μm, ~1 μm resolution — the same platform class that opened
this field in 1969 can close it.

## 4. Supporting discriminators (prism vs layers)

1. **Conservation/halo test (ontology-killer):** a prism separates, never destroys. At the
   null wavelengths the arrival missing from the disc centre must exist in angle: limb
   rings / wide aureole / fringe sidelobes. **Eclipse protocol:** during totality, measure
   off-limb brightness at 158 nm and at the far-IR minimum. Prism: anomalously bright rings
   at exactly the null wavelengths. Layers: chromospheric emission lines only, no
   continuum ring. Binary.
2. **Coherence in the nulls:** branch-cancellation nulls are organised — polarization and
   centre-to-limb phase structure. A cool layer's dip is incoherent and flat.
   Far-IR/UV spectropolarimetry of the dip regions has never been asked this question.
3. **One-function R(λ):** the apparent solar radius from metre-radio (corona-sized)
   through the optical minimum to X-ray (corona-sized again) must be threaded by ONE
   dispersion function of the displacement shell, zero per-band tuning. Anchor already
   passable: ALMA radius enlargements at 1.25 mm and 3 mm (~+0.5%, ~+1.0%).
4. **Lockstep cycle covariance:** one shell ⇒ solar-cycle changes in R(λ) and null
   positions move together across all bands. Layered atmospheres allow independent drift.
5. **Twin-null temperature identity:** current record shows FIR ~4200 K vs UV 4430 ± 50 K —
   undecided at the ±250 K level. The prism requires the two nulls to share their null
   structure; a modern FIR photometric campaign decides.

## 5. Kill conditions (pre-committed)

The prism reading is FALSIFIED if: (a) the all-angle integrated spectrum (disc + off-limb)
retains the full dip depth at the nulls with no compensating halo/ring at any angle;
(b) the nulls are shown unpolarized and incoherent at instrument sensitivity sufficient to
detect the predicted branch structure; (c) the far-IR minimum resolves at a position with
no relation to the hydrogen-family structure AND the UV geometric-mean match is shown
accidental (e.g., the true UV minimum moves off 158 nm with better between-line sampling);
(d) R(λ) demonstrably requires independent per-band mechanisms after the one-function fit
is attempted with the derived shell.

## 6. Standard-model position (rival column, stated fairly)

The layered reading explains the UV minimum via Si I/Mg I opacity sampling a ~4400 K
temperature-minimum layer (~500 km), the FIR minimum via H⁻ free-free opacity sampling the
same layer from the other side, radio sizes via plasma refraction + chromospheric/coronal
emission heights, and the radius spectrum via τ=1 surfaces. It requires the two minima to
sample the same layer coincidentally through unrelated opacity mechanisms and carries no
exact prediction for the FIR minimum position. Plasma refraction (n² = 1 − ν_p²/ν²) is the
rival's own concession that the solar atmosphere lenses radio — the fork is whether the
lensing is incidental to layered emission, or IS the spectrum.

## 7. Movement framing (SDT-native)

Every wavelength is a path length and a velocity multiplied out at c. The Sun's continuum
skeleton is hydrogen's three faces — the loose pair (H⁻ caustic), the tour at gear 4
(He II flank), the outer ladder (H14/15α flanks) — read through one displacement shell.
The dips are not cold sources: they are arrival nulls, twice-in-the-centre cancellations
of separated branches. Detectors are arrival-rate integrators (Herschel's control
thermometer, 1800, was the first proof that the separator delivers full movement into
bands the eye calls empty).
