# E108: Galactic-Floor Redshift — the Universal c-Deficit

> **Category**: Cosmology / Convergence Depth
> **Difficulty**: HIGH (frontier metrology) — but a clean SDT-only signal
> **Status**: Proposed — discriminating, no GR/ΛCDM counterpart
> **Source theory**: depth-closure z = ϟ/r, GOM05 (variable closure, c_local), E102 §6 (galactic floor)
> **Author**: James Christopher Harvey, Melbourne

---

## 1. Background — the Sun sits at a depth too

Depth-closure (GOM05) makes the local light-speed ceiling a function of position
in the lattice: `c_local(r) = c_∞(1 − z)`, `z = ϟ/r`. The Sun's *own* well gives
the solar gravitational redshift to 0.03% — but the Sun also sits inside the
**Milky Way's** depth. Treating the Galaxy's enclosed mass as a koppa:

```
z_gal = M_MW · ϟ_unit / R₀ ≈ 3.51×10⁻⁷
```

This is a **constant, isotropic** deficit of the local lattice ceiling below the
absolute `c_∞` — a **DC offset of ≈ 105 m/s** equivalent, present even in "empty"
interstellar space, **independent of any nearby mass**. Every line measured from
inside the Galaxy carries it; it is not a Doppler shift and not removable by local
gravitational accounting.

GR/ΛCDM contain no such term: the Galactic potential is a gauge offset with no
absolute reference, so a *uniform* potential produces **no** observable shift.

---

## 2. SDT Prediction

- A **universal additive redshift floor** `z_gal ≈ 3.51×10⁻⁷` (≈ 105 m/s) on
  **all** spectra of sources that share the Sun's Galactic depth — isotropic, the
  same in every sky direction, independent of the source's local environment.
- **Depth-gradient across the Galaxy**: sources at different Galactocentric radii
  `R` sit at different `z_gal(R) ∝ M(<R)/R`. Objects deeper in the Galactic
  potential (toward the centre / globular clusters near the core) carry a *larger*
  floor; halo/outskirt objects a *smaller* one — a **monotonic, located gradient**
  with Galactocentric depth, on top of (and separable from) their own dynamics.
- The reference is **absolute** (`c_∞`), so the floor is a genuine offset, not a
  relative potential difference.

---

## 3. Standard-Model Prediction

GR: only **potential differences** are observable. A uniform Galactic potential is
unobservable (gauge); a star's gravitational redshift depends only on *its own*
compactness `GM_⋆/c²R_⋆`. There is **no** universal ≈105 m/s floor and **no**
Galactocentric-depth term beyond ordinary dynamics (rotation, peculiar velocity,
the star's own surface gravity). Any common offset would be absorbed as a velocity
zero-point, not interpreted as an absolute c-deficit.

---

## 4. Observable

The signal is **not** a single line shift (degenerate with a velocity zero-point)
but the **pattern**:

| Signature | SM | SDT |
|---|---|---|
| common additive offset on all spectra | absorbable zero-point | absolute +105 m/s floor |
| variation with Galactocentric depth `M(<R)/R` | none (gauge) | monotonic gradient |
| isotropy (same all sky directions) | n/a | required |
| correlation with stellar compactness | yes (own well only) | floor is *extra*, mass-independent |

The discriminator is a **gravitational-redshift residual that scales with
Galactic depth and not with the source's own mass** — a term with the wrong
dependence to be the star's own well, and the wrong behaviour (absolute, not
relative) to be a GR potential.

---

## 5. Apparatus

- **Stellar gravitational-redshift surveys**: precision absolute redshifts of
  large stellar samples (Gaia RVS, APOGEE, Gaia-ESO) where the *own-well* term is
  modelled from each star's `M, R`; the **residual** is tested for a common floor
  and a Galactocentric-depth trend. Solar/asteroid-reflection ground-truth fixes
  the local zero-point.
- **Globular-cluster differential test**: clusters at a range of Galactocentric
  radii provide clean, co-moving stellar ensembles — compare their redshift
  residuals against `M(<R)/R`.
- **Absolute reference**: the hard part — separating an absolute floor from the
  velocity zero-point requires a depth-independent anchor (e.g. tying the scale to
  the GPS-confirmed near-field `z = ϟ/r` law, E102, and to Solar-system reflectors).

---

## 6. Feasibility

**HIGH / frontier.** The per-star precision (≈10–100 m/s absolute) is at or near
the edge of current spectroscopic zero-point control, and the chief difficulty is
the **absolute** reference (a uniform floor hides inside any velocity zero-point).
The *gradient* form (variation with Galactocentric depth) is more accessible than
the absolute floor, because it is differential across the sample. This is the
high-payoff, high-difficulty member of the depth-closure family (companion to
E102's near-Sun leg).

---

## 7. Impact

- **If a Galactocentric-depth redshift gradient is detected** (scaling as
  `M(<R)/R`, mass-independent of the source, isotropic): direct evidence the local
  lattice ceiling lies below an absolute `c_∞` — a result with **no** GR/ΛCDM
  counterpart, and the strongest confirmation of depth-closure as a real,
  position-dependent field.
- **If the residuals show no common floor and no Galactocentric trend**: the
  galactic-floor prediction is falsified at the achieved precision; depth-closure
  remains a *local* (own-well) effect only.

---

## 8. Predicted curve (reproduce)

`z_gal = M_MW·ϟ_unit/R₀ ≈ 3.51×10⁻⁷` (≈105 m/s); gradient `z_gal(R) ∝ M(<R)/R`
from the Galactic rotation/mass model (no `G`/`M` as fundamentals — `M(<R) →
v_circ²(R)·R`). Anchor to the GPS-confirmed near-field law (E102 §4) for the
absolute zero-point.
