zk2_mapper — zk^2=1 system calculator

Usage:
  zk2_mapper star <name> <z> <R_km>
  zk2_mapper orbit <star_z> <star_R_km> <planet_name> <P_days> <K_m/s>
  zk2_mapper system <star_name> <star_z> <star_R_km> [planet_name P_days K_m/s] ...

All inputs are measured quantities. 
  z     = spatial displacement redshift (lambda_obs/lambda_emit)
  R_km  = stellar radius in km
  P     = orbital period in days
  K     = star wobble semi-amplitude in m/s
