## 2. Weighted Linear Fit: H0 = m × log10(z_max) + b

```
Slope m     = -0.7919 ± 0.2342 km/s/Mpc per decade
Intercept b = 70.1892 ± 0.6223 km/s/Mpc
R²          = 0.487940
```

The slope is **NEGATIVE** (-0.792 ± 0.234).
This is 3.4 sigma from zero.
**SIGNIFICANT**: H0 genuinely decreases with survey depth.
This is CONSISTENT with the SDT prediction.
