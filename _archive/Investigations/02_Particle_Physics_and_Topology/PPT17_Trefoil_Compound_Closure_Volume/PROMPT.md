# PPT17 — Trefoil Compound Closure and the 6π⁵ Volume Assignment

> **Author:** J. C. Harvey, Melbourne. **Opened:** 2026-08-14.  
> **Status:** PRE-REGISTERED before instrumentation.  
> **Scope:** investigation-level only; no `laws.hpp` or `Laws/` edit.

## Question

Does the physical trefoil recurrence

> one contrarotation, two poloidal transfers, and `n` rotations, repeated through
> six compound phases before the original configuration returns

produce the proposed displacement-volume ratio `6π⁵` without consuming the
measured proton/electron mass ratio?

The test separates four statements that existing records conflate:

1. `m_p/m_e = V_disp,p/V_disp,e` — Law IV identity.
2. A `(2,3)` trefoil has six distinct compound phases before recurrence.
3. Those phases carry five independent circular measures.
4. Their native measure is exactly `6π⁵`, rather than `Cπ^d` with an
   unestablished coefficient, exponent, or quotient.

Only statements 2–4 can pay the mass-ratio ledger. Statement 1 cannot.

## Frozen inputs

- Trefoil winding pair `(p,q)=(2,3)`.
- Electron comparison `(p,q)=(1,1)`.
- Compound phase index `j`.
- Discrete phase state
  `S_j = ((2j mod 6), (3j mod 6), (-1)^j)`, where the final component records
  contrarotational parity.
- Mechanical channel count supplied for this investigation:
  one contrarotation channel, two poloidal-transfer channels, and `n`
  rotational channels.
- `π` and integer arithmetic only in the native construction.
- `sdt::laws::law_VI::mass_ratio::measured_ratio` may be opened only for the
  final correspondence comparison.

No measured particle mass, measured mass ratio, `V_disp_e`, `V_disp_p`,
exclusion radius, Compton wavelength, or fitted wake scale may determine `n`,
an angular period, or a quotient.

## Pre-registered routes

### Route A — recurrence count

Enumerate `S_j` from `j=0` until the full state returns. Report separately:

- distinct toroidal/poloidal phase pairs;
- contrarotational parity;
- first return of the complete compound state.

**A gate:** exactly six distinct pre-return states and first full return at
`j=6`. Otherwise the six-phase construction is inconsistent.

### Route B — dimensional count

The declared compound event contains `d(n)=1+2+n=3+n` circular measures.
Evaluate `n=0..6` in one pass.

**B gate:** the exponent five is derived only if an upstream mechanical rule,
independent of the target ratio, fixes `n=2`. Merely observing that `n=2`
makes `d=5` is a target-conditioned solution and remains PENDING.

### Route C — angular-domain measure

For every `n=0..6`, enumerate the full pre-registered domain family:

- ordinary angular period `2π`;
- axis-identified half-domain `π`;
- framed/spinorial period `4π`;
- no quotient and the complete powers-of-two quotient family generated by
  independently identifying any declared channel by a half-turn.

No member may be selected after comparison. The output reports the complete
equivalence class `Cπ^d`.

**C gate:** `6π⁵` is DERIVED only if the mechanics uniquely fixes:

- `n=2`;
- five independent measures;
- their periods/identifications;
- net coefficient `C=6`;

before the measured ratio is opened. Multiple admissible constructions,
or a coefficient chosen from the target, leave `π⁵` PENDING.

### Route D — downstream correspondence

After A–C are sealed, compare:

- the uniquely generated value, if one exists;
- `6π⁵`;
- the measured `m_p/m_e`.

Report signed residual in ppm. This is correspondence only and cannot change
the A–C disposition.

## Rejection criteria

- The six-phase claim is falsified if Route A does not first return at six.
- The five-measure claim remains PENDING if `n=2` is not independently fixed.
- The `6π⁵` displacement-volume assignment remains PENDING if the angular
  normalization or coefficient is non-unique.
- Any use of the measured ratio to choose `n`, a period, a quotient, or a
  coefficient invalidates the run.

## Possible outcomes

1. **Full derivation:** six phases, five measures, and coefficient six are all
   independently forced; `6π⁵` is generated before comparison.
2. **Partial payment:** six-phase recurrence is established, while `π⁵` or
   its coefficient remains underdetermined.
3. **Exclusion:** the compound recurrence itself does not close as stated.
