║  PART 2: DOES EXCITATION SLOW THE WHOLE ATOM?              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  Movement budget: v_circ² + v_trans² = c²
  In bound state: v_orbital IS v_trans (the electron's
  circulation around the nucleus is its translational
  velocity in the atomic frame).

  v_circ = internal vortex circulation of the electron.
  When electron is excited: v_orbital decreases
  (SDT orbital velocity law: wider slot = lower velocity).
  Therefore v_circ INCREASES (movement budget conservation).

  But the ORBITAL angular momentum L = m_e × v × r decreases?
  Let's check. Bohr: v_n ∝ 1/n, r_n ∝ n², so L_n ∝ n.
  ANGULAR MOMENTUM INCREASES with excitation level.

  Hydrogen ground → excited:
    n         v/c    v_circ/c       r(pm)      L(ℏ)    ΔL/L₁
