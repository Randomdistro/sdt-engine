 PHASE 1  Lattice kinetic theory of viscosity
------------------------------------------------------------

 relay tick = momentum flux; coefficient is mu. Lattice kinetic
 theory gives   mu = (1/3) rho lambda v_relay  =>  nu = mu/rho

   of x,y,z carries flux across the plane) -- the SAME 1/3 as
   P_cf = P_conv/3 in laws.hpp.  DERIVED (geometry), CALIBRATED(0).
