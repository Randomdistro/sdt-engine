 VERDICT
  - One pinhole well yields kW-scale net power; it goes net-POSITIVE from a
    ~hot gradient up (>=50 C/km). A normal 30 C/km / 2 km site is too cold to
    flash useful steam -> use an ORC working fluid or a deeper/hotter hole.
  - The narrow 'pinhole' bore is the dominant penalty: pump friction ~1/d^5;
    below ~40 mm the friction eats the output. Thermosiphon buoyancy offsets part.
  - Turbine design point is self-consistent: b_opt near Tesla's measured gap and
    operating rpm well under the Inconel hoop-stress limit (F5 satisfied).
