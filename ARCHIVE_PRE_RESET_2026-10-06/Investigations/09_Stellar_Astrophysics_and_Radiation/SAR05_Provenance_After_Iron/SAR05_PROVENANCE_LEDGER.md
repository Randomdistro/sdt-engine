## 2 · The NP19 Two-Ledger tie (neutronisation row, row 4)

yet **exothermic** at *event scale* (the EC valve through which ~3×10⁴⁶ J of gravitational

(electron-degeneracy support evaporating — the run on the banks), **not** an energy runaway:
directed spation pressure pays, ~99% venting as neutrinos. No stored gravitational-potential
reservoir is spent; gravity is the *directed pressure of space*, and the "supply" is the
in-fall reorganising, not a beyond-CMB wire.
