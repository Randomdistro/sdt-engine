## Target

White dwarfs shrink as they gain mass — the inverse mass–radius relation — up to the 1.4 M☉
limit beyond which no white dwarf exists. Measured anchors: Sirius B (1.018 M☉, 0.0084 R☉)
and the Gaia white-dwarf sequence.

NP30 §11 (compression floor — the same physics one density regime up, which landed the
neutron-star 11.0 km), reopen R4 (the seat-stack counting run).
