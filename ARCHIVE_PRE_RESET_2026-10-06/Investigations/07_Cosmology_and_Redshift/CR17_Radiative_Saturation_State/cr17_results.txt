[5] ANCHOR SENSITIVITY - how hard does the answer depend on the band choice?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  All three outputs are pure powers of lambda_sat:
      T ~ 1/lambda ,  N ~ 1/lambda ,  v ~ lambda ,  P_rad ~ 1/lambda^4
  so a factor f error in the anchor gives f in T, N and v, and f^4 in P_rad.
  Across the full near-IR band (780 -> 1400 nm, factor 1.795):
      T, N, v vary by  x1.79      P_rad varies by  x10.4
  -> v_sat and T_sat are robust to the band choice; P_rad is NOT (it is the
     quantity that most needs the saturation wavelength pinned by mechanism.

  For reference only, NOT used as an anchor: CR13's lambda_emit = 974.7 nm gives
      T = 2973.0 K , N = 1090.8 , v = 2.7484e+05 m/s , P_rad = 1.9702e-02 Pa
  That N reproduces the imported redshift because 974.7 nm was BACK-COMPUTED from it
  via Wien. It is a restatement of the input, not a check on it. Flagged, not used.
