=== REGIME STRUCTURE ===

   BULGE (< 3.5 kpc): spheroid of known engines.
     Each engine occludes convergence for all others.
     Accumulated Koppa grows with enclosed engines.
     Exterior engines (toward rim) also contribute.

   TRANSITION (3.5 - 5 kpc): bulge Koppa is the floor.
     Bar terminus + arm roots add their occlusion.
     Bonding geometry (CQ19b) locks bar-arm junction.

   DISK (5 - 25 kpc): annular shells, step by step.
     Each shell adds its engines to accumulated total.
     Exterior shells occlude convergence from rim.
     Recalculate at each step — depends on what came before.

   The flat rotation curve is the natural output of
   bidirectional occlusion in a disk geometry.
   Interior engines reduce convergence from centre.
   Exterior engines reduce convergence from rim.
   Both contribute to the net gradient at every radius.
