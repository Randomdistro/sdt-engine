READING:
 - Schmidt (moment = ONE nucleon's intrinsic spin g_s) works to ~few-% ONLY when
   the nucleus IS one nucleon outside a closed shell (O-17, Pb-207, F-19, He-3).
 - As the arrangement becomes collective, the moment QUENCHES toward the interior.
 - The moment tracks the ARRANGEMENT (single-particle vs collective), not a fixed
   intrinsic spin. Standard rescue = config-mixing + meson-exchange currents =
   admitting the moment is the WHOLE configuration's circulation, not one spin.
