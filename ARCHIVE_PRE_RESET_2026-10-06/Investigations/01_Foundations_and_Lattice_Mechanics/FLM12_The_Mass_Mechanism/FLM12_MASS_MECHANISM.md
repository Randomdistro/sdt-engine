## 0. Why this document exists (the confinement objection was the clue)

[FLM12's predecessor work] tried to give the neutron and the deuteron their mass via a **localised
internal electron** (a seated point charge). That failed a hard test: localising an electron to
femtometre scale costs `ℏc/Δx ≈ 100–700 MeV`, `50–300×` the `1–2 MeV` bindings it was meant to
supply — the 1930s *"no electrons in the nucleus"* objection.

The objection is not a wall; it is a **signpost**. It proves, cleanly, that **mass cannot be a
substance localised in a carrier** — because any localised carrier pays a confinement cost that
dwarfs the mass it is supposed to provide. Under SDT strictures there is only one thing mass can then
be: **a property of the surrounding field, not of a carrier inside the form.** This document
determines that mechanism and then tests it against every mass-relevant anomaly in CODATA.

## PART I — THE MECHANISM

### 1. The claim (one sentence)

**Mass is the spation field's resistance to a change in a form's vector; the resistor is the
convergent phase, arriving isotropically at `c` in discrete quanta, and mass is the coefficient by
which that phase pushes back when the form is accelerated.** Nothing carries mass; a form *commands*
mass, in proportion to how much of the incoming phase it engages. **[NATIVE — this is the SDT
ontology, forced by §0.]**

### 2. Step-by-step derivation

**Step 1 — The convergent phase (Law I).** Every spation relays phase to its neighbours once per
Planck tick `t_P = ℓ_P/c`, in discrete quanta `ε` (Law I: `Φ = Nε`, `P_conv`). Every point in space
is therefore bathed in an **isotropic influx** delivered at `c` — "the incoming phase at the speed of
light in minute discrete quantisation." This is not a force yet; it is the medium's ordinary
operation. **[NATIVE — Law I.]**

**Step 2 — A form at rest feels no net force (recovers Newton I).** A form (a persistent winding —
`W=1` unknot, `W=3` trefoil) sits in the influx. At rest the incoming quanta arrive **equally from
all directions**; the delivered momentum cancels; net force `= 0`. Rest costs nothing. Inertia is
invisible until you try to **change the vector**. This is Newton's first law, *derived*, not posited.
**[NATIVE.]**

**Step 3 — Changing the vector breaks the symmetry (recovers Newton II, `F = ma`).** Accelerate the
form and, in its own frame, the influx is no longer isotropic: quanta arrive **denser/blue-relayed
from ahead**, **sparser/red-relayed from behind** (a relay-Doppler of the discrete influx). The net
delivered-momentum imbalance **opposes the change**, growing with the acceleration:

```
F_resist  =  (rate of incoming phase-momentum imbalance)  ∝  a
          ≡  m · a
```

The proportionality constant **is the mass** — a measure of how strongly the incoming phase resists
the form's change of vector. It lives in **the field's response**, scaled by the form; it is not
stored in the form. **[NATIVE — this is the mechanical content the framework was missing: inertia =
relay-Doppler imbalance of the discrete influx under acceleration.]**

**Step 4 — Magnitude = how much field the form engages (Law IV).** The size of the imbalance is set
by how much incoming phase the form intercepts and must re-relay per tick — its
throughput-reorganisation, Law IV's `V_disp`:

```
m  =  (Φ / c²) · V_disp / (3 ℓ_P³)          [Law IV / T5, now mechanised]
```

Law IV already *said* "mass = reorganisation cost." Step 3 says **to whom the cost is paid** (the
incoming phase) and **why it resists** (the acceleration-induced imbalance). Law IV supplies the
coefficient; Steps 1–3 supply the mechanism. **[RE-DESCRIPTION of Law IV + NATIVE mechanism for it.]**

**Step 5 — `E = mc²` is automatic.** The form's *total* engagement of the convergent relay, expressed
as energy, is `mc²`: the rest-energy is the whole field-engagement of the form standing still. There
is no separate "energy reservoir" — mass and energy are **one field-engagement read two ways**
(resistance `m`, throughput `E`). The interchangeability is not a brute fact to accept; it is the
obvious consequence of one substrate measured by its grip and by its flow (distillation §2.2).
**[DISSOLVED — the "mass–energy interchange without a mechanism" complaint.]**

**Step 6 — Inertial mass = gravitational mass (equivalence principle as a *theorem*).** Two readings
of one quantity:
- **inertial** mass = the influx resisting the form's *acceleration* (Steps 3–4);
- **gravitational** mass = the form's *occlusion* of the same influx (Law III), which casts the shadow
  other forms fall down.

Both are **"how much incoming phase the form engages."** They are the **same number by construction**,
so `m_inertial ≡ m_grav` **identically** — not by the astonishing coincidence GR must postulate. This
is the mechanism's sharpest, already-tested prediction (MICROSCOPE 2022: `η < 10⁻¹⁵`). **[NATIVE —
the strongest falsifiable win here; see Part II §A1.]**

**Step 7 — Mass and flux are one axis (the two-ledger lemma).** Mass = the field **gripping** the
form (resistance to being shaped/moved, arrow field→form). Magnetic flux = the form **moving** the
field (circulation displacing spation, arrow form→field). *Same interface, opposite arrow.* So a
form's magnetic moment and its mass are the **same field-engagement read forwards and backwards** —

framing; RE-DESCRIPTION at the level of numbers — see §B.]**

**Step 8 — Bonds, synchronies, harmonics (the closing line, made precise).** *"All bonds, all
synchronies, all harmonics are the spation field doing what it does."* Two forms **bond** when their
field-engagements **overlap and phase-lock** (synchronise). The joint form re-relays the **shared**

engagements:

```
mass defect  =  Δ(total field-engagement)  =  engagement(joint form) − Σ engagement(parts)
binding energy  =  the field-resistance SAVED (or added) by the synchrony
```

- A mass defect is therefore **not** "energy stored/released"; it is the **change in field-resistance**

**Step 9 — The confinement objection DISSOLVES.** It assumed a localised carrier whose momentum you
