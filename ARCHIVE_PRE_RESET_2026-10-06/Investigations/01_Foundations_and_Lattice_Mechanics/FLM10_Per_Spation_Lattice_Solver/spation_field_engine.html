Field

The proton is the 6π trefoil: thread length = 6π·R_p = 15.85 fm, chord width 5.8×10⁻²⁰ m — true aspect ≈ 273,000 : 1, so the thread is thickened here to be seen at all. Each frame every spation is pushed out of the nearest point on the thread by d→√(d²+R²), so the field parts around the knot and closes behind it as it moves — water. Colour-shells hug the thread; the pulse drives them inward onto the proton (the convergence feeding the engine). The dipole slider adds the leading-compression / trailing-rarefaction of the moving occluder = inertia.

The rotation ⇄ cord length slider is an exchange: wind in rotation and the cord pays out length — it spins faster and contracts. As it spins to a blur it pulls into the donut (the trefoil's spun-fast average) with a phased toroidal flow (2 toroidal × 3 poloidal = the 6π winding); the field then parts around the smooth torus instead of the thread.
