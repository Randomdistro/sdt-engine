## Findings

### F1 — the c "spectroscopic route" is an identity, not an extraction (cuts against)

`c = cR∞[Hz] / R∞[m⁻¹]` returns 299 792 457.99999 — c to 7 parts in 10⁹ —
because CODATA **tabulates cR∞ as c × R∞**. The ratio cannot fail. Tag
should read IDENTITY, not EXTRACTED.

Consequence for the README: the claim that the spectroscopic route "stands as
primary" against the withdrawn Mercury closure does not survive. Since 1983 the
metre is *defined* from c, so no c-free wavelength measurement exists and there
is no independent spectroscopic extraction of c to be had. The correct standing
is the one the printed note already half-states: **c is a unit bridge, and its
physical content is that ONE relay speed closes every line** — a consistency
statement. The line through c stays drawn; the reason given must change.

### F2 — item 8 is scored against retired ballparks (cuts *for* SDT)

The T_CMB item compares the CR13 chain to `measured::T_rec = 3000.0` and
`measured::z_rec = 1100.0`. Both are round textbook epoch-frame numbers — the
same two flagged and replaced in `Benchmarks/neutrino_investigation.cpp` earlier
today. Re-scored against the measured-consistent anchor:

| anchor | value | CR13 chain (2972.99 K) deviates |
|---|---|---|
| `measured::T_rec` (round) | 3000.0 K | −0.900% |
| T_CMB × (1 + 1089) | 2970.79 K | **+0.074%** |

**A factor of 12 better against the measured-consistent anchor.** The item is being penalised
by a stale reference, not by its own physics.

**Canon proposal (awaiting Harvey's explicit word — no edit made).** `T_rec` and
`z_rec` sit inside `namespace measured`, which the ruleset defines as the
whitelist of measured facts (CODATA / IAU / FIRAS). A round 3000 K and a round
1100 are not measured facts. Either replace with the FIRAS-frame z = 1089 plus a
computed T, or move both out of `measured` into a labelled epoch-frame block.
Every consumer of those two symbols inherits the error until this is settled.

### F3 — the reduced whitelist under-counts by one length

The routes that draw the lines through the masses consume lengths that do not
appear in the printed reduced set:

- `m_e = ℏ/(αc·a₀)` consumes **a₀** — no derived route for a₀ exists in
  `laws.hpp` (checked); it is a measured input.
- `m_p = 4ℏ/(R_p·c)` consumes **R_p** — measured (muonic hydrogen).

The dossier is locally correct that R_p ↔ m_p is *one* fact rather than two (the
0.02% residual is shared). So the correct reduced set is:

Five inputs, not four. And this names the payoff precisely: closing **a₀** (the
