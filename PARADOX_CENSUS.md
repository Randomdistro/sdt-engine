## 0. What counts as a paradox (so this isn't rigged)

I separate four severities, and I label every entry:

- **[LOGICAL]** — an internal contradiction: the framework's own axioms imply both X and ¬X.
- **[SELF-LIMITING]** — the theory predicts its own breakdown (infinities, singularities).
- **[FINE-TUNING]** — a quantity must be set to absurd precision with no reason inside the theory.
- **[EMPIRICAL]** — an observation the framework cannot accommodate without bolt-ons.
- **[INTERPRETATIONAL]** — empirically-equivalent stories the theory cannot decide between.

A **paradox** proper is [LOGICAL] or [SELF-LIMITING]. The rest are *crises* — still fatal to a
"finished theory" claim, but not contradictions. I keep the distinction because at the end I
hold SDT to exactly the same ruler. A gap is not a paradox; I will not let SDT's gaps be
counted as paradoxes, and I will not let the others' paradoxes be downgraded to gaps.

**The thesis in one line:** almost every paradox below is *manufactured by a specific
premise* — a wavefunction that must collapse, a field that must self-energise, an attraction
that must be sourced by mass, a continuum with no shortest length, a vacuum that must carry
energy. SDT does not hold those premises, so the paradoxes have nothing to grow from. That is
not the same as "SDT has answered every question" — see §8, which is the honest part.

## 1. Special Relativity

SR's "paradoxes" are mostly resolved *within* SR — but every resolution costs an ontological
commitment (no preferred frame, relativity of simultaneity, the block universe) that is itself
contested. They exist because SR **abolishes the substrate** and makes simultaneity frame-relative.

1. **Twin paradox** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Two twins, symmetric by the principle of relativity,
   yet one ages less. *Why it exists:* SR forbids a preferred frame, so the asymmetry must be
   smuggled back in via acceleration/proper-time — which feels like patching the very symmetry
   the theory asserted. Resolved operationally; the ontological "who really moved?" has no
   answer in SR by construction.
2. **Pole-and-barn (ladder) paradox** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. A pole fits in a shorter barn in one
   frame, doesn't in the other. *Why:* relativity of simultaneity — "both doors shut at once"
   is frame-dependent. No contradiction, but it means *length and simultaneity have no
   observer-independent fact*.
3. **Bell's spaceship paradox** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Does a thread between two identically
   accelerated ships break? (It does.) *Why:* widespread confusion because SR has no substrate
   to say what "the same acceleration" means physically; took the community decades.
4. **Ehrenfest paradox** [LOGICAL, within rigid-body SR]. A rigidly rotating disk: circumference
   Lorentz-contracts but radius doesn't → C/2R ≠ π → non-Euclidean. *Why:* SR + Born rigidity
   are mutually inconsistent for rotation. This contradiction is genuine and historically drove
   Einstein toward curved spacetime. SR alone cannot describe a spun-up rigid disk.
5. **Conventionality of simultaneity (Reichenbach–Grünbaum)** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Only the
   *two-way* speed of light is measurable; the one-way speed is a convention (the ε parameter).
   *Why:* with no preferred frame, clock synchronisation has no unique fact — c-isotropy is
   partly stipulated, not measured.
6. **Rietdijk–Putnam block universe** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Relativity of simultaneity →
   different observers' "nows" slice spacetime differently → all events equally real →
   no objective passage of time. *Why:* directly forced by frame-relative simultaneity. SR
   cannot host an objective present.

## 2. General Relativity

GR's paradoxes are heavier — several are [SELF-LIMITING] (the theory forecasts its own death)
and one ([LOGICAL] with QM) is the deepest open problem in physics. They exist because GR makes
**spacetime itself dynamical and continuous, with gravity as geometry and no shortest length.**

1. **Spacetime singularities** [SELF-LIMITING]. Penrose–Hawking theorems prove GR generically
   produces points of infinite curvature/density (black-hole cores, the Big Bang). *Why:* a
   continuum with attractive gravity and no minimum length has nothing to stop collapse. GR
   predicts where GR stops being true.
2. **Black-hole information paradox** [LOGICAL, GR+QM]. Hawking radiation is exactly thermal →
   an object's information vanishes when the hole evaporates → pure states evolve to mixed →
   QM unitarity is violated. *Why:* the event horizon (a one-way causal membrane) + the no-hair
   theorem + thermal Hawking emission jointly forbid information from getting out. A genuine
   contradiction between GR and QM, unresolved for 50 years.
3. **AMPS firewall paradox** [LOGICAL]. Saving unitarity (entangling early and late Hawking
   quanta) violates the monogamy of entanglement unless the horizon is a wall of high-energy
   quanta — which violates the equivalence principle ("nothing special at the horizon").
   *Why:* sharpens #2 — you must break QM, the equivalence principle, or locality. Pick one.
4. **The problem of time** [LOGICAL]. Canonical quantisation of GR (Wheeler–DeWitt) gives
   Ĥ|ψ⟩ = 0 — the wavefunction of the universe does not evolve; time disappears. *Why:* GR's
   diffeomorphism invariance has no external time, but QM needs one. The two notions of time
   are incompatible.
5. **Non-localisability of gravitational energy** [LOGICAL-ish]. There is no covariant local
   energy density for the gravitational field; "energy" is carried by coordinate-dependent
   pseudotensors, and global energy conservation fails in general (no timelike Killing vector).
   *Why:* the equivalence principle lets you transform the field away locally, so its energy
   cannot live at a point.
6. **Closed timelike curves** [LOGICAL]. GR admits exact solutions (Gödel, Kerr interior,
   traversable wormholes, Tipler cylinder) containing time loops → grandfather paradox. *Why:*
   the field equations constrain local geometry but not global causal structure; nothing in GR
   forbids CTCs.
7. **The hole argument** [INTERPRETATIONAL→resolved]. Diffeomorphism invariance implies bare
   spacetime points carry no identity (else the theory would be indeterministic). *Why:* forces
   a relational reading of spacetime; "where" has no substantival meaning.
8. **Strong cosmic censorship / Cauchy-horizon breakdown** [SELF-LIMITING]. Inside charged/
   rotating holes, determinism can fail at the inner horizon. *Why:* GR's predictivity is not
   guaranteed beyond a Cauchy horizon.
9. **Unfulfilled Mach's principle** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Einstein wanted inertia sourced by all
   other matter; GR keeps absolute local inertial frames (and rotation relative to "nothing" in
   asymptotically flat space). *Why:* GR did not deliver the relational inertia that motivated it.
10. **Initial low-entropy / Weyl-curvature problem** [FINE-TUNING]. The Big Bang was
    extraordinarily smooth (low gravitational entropy) for no reason GR supplies. *Why:* GR's
    equations are time-symmetric; the arrow of time must be put in by hand as a special initial state.

## 3. Quantum Mechanics

QM's paradoxes are the densest, and the central one ([LOGICAL]) has no agreed solution after a
century. They exist because QM posits a **wavefunction that evolves unitarily and continuously,
yet yields single definite outcomes by a discontinuous, unexplained collapse.**

1. **The measurement problem** [LOGICAL]. Schrödinger evolution is linear and deterministic and
   keeps superpositions; experiments give one outcome. The two cannot both be exhaustive. *Why:*
   the theory has *two* evolution laws (unitary U + collapse R) and no rule, inside the theory,
   for when R fires. This is the root paradox; everything below is a face of it.
2. **Schrödinger's cat** [LOGICAL]. Unitary evolution scales to a |dead⟩+|alive⟩ macro-state.
   *Why:* linearity has no built-in cutoff between micro and macro.
3. **Wigner's friend & Frauchiger–Renner** [LOGICAL]. Recent no-go theorems: if QM applies to
   observers, rational agents using QM derive *contradictory facts* about the same event. *Why:*
   QM cannot consistently model its own use by multiple observers without dropping one of
   {universality, single-world, non-contextual facts}.
4. **EPR** [LOGICAL/INTERPRETATIONAL]. Entangled measurements are perfectly correlated at a
   distance → either QM is incomplete or reality is nonlocal. *Why:* the entangled state is
   non-separable; a local-realistic completion is demanded by EPR's reality criterion.
5. **Bell's theorem** [EMPIRICAL+LOGICAL]. Experiment violates Bell inequalities → **no** local
   hidden-variable theory can reproduce QM. *Why:* nature's correlations exceed any local-causal
   bound. You must give up locality or definite pre-measurement values.
6. **Wave–particle duality** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. One entity shows interference (wave) and
   localised hits (particle). *Why:* the formalism is neither; complementarity forbids a single
   spacetime picture.
7. **Quantum Zeno paradox** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Continuous observation freezes evolution.
   *Why:* repeated projection (collapse) keeps re-preparing the initial state.
8. **Delayed-choice / quantum eraser** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. A present choice appears to fix past
   behaviour. *Why:* with no realist trajectory, "what the photon did" isn't defined until
   measured — looks retrocausal.
9. **Born-rule unexplained** [LOGICAL-gap]. Why P = |ψ|²? It is an *axiom*, not derived from
   unitary dynamics (Gleason/decision-theoretic "derivations" smuggle assumptions). *Why:*
   probabilities are bolted onto an otherwise deterministic formalism.
10. **Preferred-basis problem** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Decoherence picks a basis only given a
    system/environment split that the theory doesn't supply fundamentally.
11. **Kochen–Specker contextuality** [LOGICAL]. Observables cannot be assigned
    measurement-independent values consistently. *Why:* the algebra of projectors forbids
    non-contextual value maps.
12. **The interpretation underdetermination** [INTERPRETATIONAL]. Copenhagen, Many-Worlds,
    Bohm, GRW, QBism, Relational — empirically identical, ontologically irreconcilable. *Why:*
    the formalism is silent about what exists, so the ontology floats free.

## 4. Quantum Field Theory & the Standard Model

The SM is the most predictive theory ever built and *also* a museum of fine-tunings and
unexplained inputs. They exist because the SM **postulates fundamental fields with
free couplings, a fundamental scalar Higgs, and a continuum (hence divergences).**

1. **Hierarchy/naturalness problem** [FINE-TUNING]. The Higgs mass gets quantum corrections
   ∝ the cutoff²; keeping it at 125 GeV needs cancellation to ~1 part in 10³⁴. *Why:* a
   *fundamental scalar* has no symmetry protecting its mass. (LHC found no SUSY to fix it →
   the crisis deepened.)
2. **Cosmological constant problem** [FINE-TUNING, worst in physics]. QFT vacuum energy exceeds
   the observed Λ by ~10¹²⁰. *Why:* every field's zero-point energy gravitates; nothing cancels
   it. (Shared with §7.)
3. **Strong-CP problem** [FINE-TUNING]. The QCD θ-term should be O(1) but |θ| < 10⁻¹⁰ (no
   neutron EDM). *Why:* nothing in QCD sets θ to zero; needs an invented axion.
4. **Neutrino mass** [EMPIRICAL]. The SM predicts massless neutrinos; oscillations prove mass.
   *Why:* requires bolt-on right-handed neutrinos / seesaw; Dirac-vs-Majorana unknown.
5. **Baryon asymmetry** [EMPIRICAL]. The universe is matter, not 50/50. SM CP violation is ~10
   orders too small. *Why:* the Sakharov conditions aren't met by SM physics.
6. **~19–26 free parameters** [FINE-TUNING/incompleteness]. Masses, mixing angles, couplings —
   all *measured, none derived*. *Why:* the SM is a framework, not a first-principles theory;
   the numbers are inputs. (This is precisely the fitting the SDT programme refuses.)
7. **Three generations** [incompleteness]. Why three copies? No reason.
8. **Fermion mass hierarchy** [FINE-TUNING]. Yukawas span ~10⁶ (electron→top) with no pattern.
9. **Charge quantisation** [incompleteness]. Why is |q_proton| exactly |q_electron|? The SM
   does not require it (anomaly cancellation hints, but the generation/charge assignment is
   put in). *Why:* no topological reason inside the SM.
10. **Quark confinement / Yang–Mills mass gap** [LOGICAL-unproven]. No free quark has ever been
    seen, and the existence of the mass gap is an *unproven* Millennium Problem. *Why:* the
    non-perturbative regime of the theory is not analytically controlled.
11. **Landau pole / triviality** [SELF-LIMITING]. The U(1) coupling diverges at finite energy;
    QED (and φ⁴) may be trivial as continuum theories. *Why:* a continuum with running couplings
    has no guaranteed high-energy completion.
12. **Renormalisation infinities** [SELF-LIMITING, tamed]. Bare quantities are infinite;
    physics is extracted by subtraction. *Why:* point particles + a continuum → divergent
    self-energies. Pragmatically cured, conceptually disliked (Dirac, Feynman both said so).
13. **Vacuum metastability** [FINE-TUNING]. The measured Higgs/top masses put our vacuum near
    the edge of metastable. *Why:* the Higgs potential's shape is an accident of the parameters.
14. **Gravity excluded** [incompleteness]. The SM has no gravity at all.
15. **Proton spin "crisis"** [EMPIRICAL]. Quark spins sum to ~30% of the proton's. *Why:* the
    constituent picture doesn't cleanly account for spin.

## 5. String Theory

String theory's paradoxes are mostly [INTERPRETATIONAL]/methodological — it risks
unfalsifiability. They exist because the theory **buys finiteness and unification with extra
dimensions, supersymmetry, and an astronomical space of vacua.**

1. **The landscape** [methodological]. ~10⁵⁰⁰ (or more) consistent vacua, no principle to pick
   ours → essentially any low-energy physics is "allowed." *Why:* compactifying 6–7 extra
   dimensions has combinatorially many choices (fluxes, Calabi–Yau topologies).
2. **Unfalsifiability** [methodological]. No distinctive prediction at any reachable energy.
   *Why:* the string scale is ~Planckian; the landscape erases sharp predictions.
3. **Unobserved extra dimensions** [EMPIRICAL]. 10 or 11 dimensions; we see 4. *Why:* requires
   compactification whose details are unfixed (the moduli problem).
4. **Unobserved supersymmetry** [EMPIRICAL]. Required for the superstring; the LHC found no
   superpartners. *Why:* SUSY must be broken at an unspecified, ever-rising scale.
5. **Moduli stabilisation** [incompleteness]. The sizes/shapes of extra dimensions are massless
   fields that must be frozen by hand (KKLT etc., themselves contested).
6. **Background dependence** [LOGICAL-ish]. Perturbative string theory is defined *on* a fixed
   background spacetime — in tension with GR's background independence. *Why:* no complete
   non-perturbative (M-theory) definition exists.
7. **de Sitter problem / swampland** [EMPIRICAL+methodological]. AdS/CFT works for negative Λ;
   our universe has positive Λ, and stable dS vacua are hard/maybe-impossible to build. *Why:*
   the controllable corner of the theory is the wrong-signed cosmology.

## 6. MOND

MOND is the nearest mainstream relative of SDT (both modify the low-acceleration regime), so
its paradoxes are diagnostic. They exist because MOND is **a phenomenological force law with a
hand-inserted scale and no native relativistic/cosmological substrate.**

1. **a₀ is fitted by hand** [FINE-TUNING]. The acceleration scale a₀ ≈ 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² is a free
   constant — and its numerical near-equality with cH₀/2π is an *unexplained* coincidence.
   *Why:* MOND offers a force law, not a derivation of its own scale.
2. **No working relativistic completion / GW speed** [EMPIRICAL, near-fatal]. GW170817 fixed the
   speed of gravity = c to ~10⁻¹⁵; this killed the extra-field (TeVeS-type) MOND completions
   that needed gravity ≠ c. *Why:* MOND's relativistic versions added fields that disperse GWs.
3. **Galaxy clusters still need dark mass** [EMPIRICAL]. MOND under-predicts cluster dynamics by
   ~2× — it does not remove the missing-mass problem at cluster scale.
4. **The Bullet Cluster** [EMPIRICAL]. Lensing mass is offset from the baryonic gas — the
   textbook argument *against* pure force-law modification.
5. **External field effect / SEP violation** [LOGICAL-ish]. In MOND a system's internal
   dynamics depend on the external field it sits in — violating the strong equivalence
   principle. *Why:* the nonlinearity is in acceleration, not tidal field.
6. **No cosmology** [incompleteness]. MOND does not by itself give the CMB acoustic peaks, BBN,
   or structure growth.

## 7. ΛCDM (standard cosmology)

ΛCDM fits the CMB and large-scale structure superbly and pays for it with a **95% dark sector
that has never been seen** plus a growing list of tensions. They exist because the model
**explains anomalies by adding invisible components (Λ, CDM, inflaton) rather than changing the
gravitational law.**

1. **The cosmological constant problem** [FINE-TUNING]. As §4.2: Λ is 10¹²⁰ below the QFT
   expectation, *and* the coincidence problem — why is Λ comparable to the matter density *now*?
2. **Dark matter undetected** [EMPIRICAL]. 27% of the universe; 40 years of direct-detection
   (XENON, LUX, LZ) and collider searches return null. *Why:* the particle is inferred only
   gravitationally; no non-gravitational handle has appeared.
3. **Dark energy unexplained** [EMPIRICAL]. 68% of the universe; no microphysics, just w ≈ −1.
4. **The Hubble tension** [EMPIRICAL, >5σ]. Local distance-ladder H₀ ≈ 73 vs CMB-inferred
   ≈ 67 km/s/Mpc. *Why:* the early- and late-universe calibrations disagree beyond error.
5. **The S₈/σ₈ tension** [EMPIRICAL]. Weak-lensing/clustering finds *less* structure than the
   CMB predicts.
6. **Small-scale CDM crises** [EMPIRICAL]. (a) **core–cusp** — halos are observed cored, CDM
   predicts cusps; (b) **missing satellites** — far fewer dwarfs than simulated; (c)
   **too-big-to-fail** — the biggest predicted subhalos have no bright counterparts;
   (d) **planes of satellites** — MW, M31, Cen A satellites lie in thin co-rotating planes,
   astronomically unlikely for random CDM debris. *Why:* collisionless cold dark matter
   over-predicts substructure and the wrong density profiles.
7. **CMB large-angle anomalies** [EMPIRICAL]. Low quadrupole; the "axis of evil" (aligned
   low multipoles); the Cold Spot; hemispherical power asymmetry. *Why:* statistical isotropy is
   mildly but persistently violated.
8. **Primordial lithium problem** [EMPIRICAL]. BBN predicts ~3× the Li-7 actually observed.
9. **Horizon & flatness problems** [FINE-TUNING]. The CMB is uniform across regions that were
   never in causal contact, and Ω is fine-tuned to 1. *Why:* standard expansion can't smooth or
   flatten — so **inflation** was invented, which then carries *its own* problems:
   the measure problem, eternal inflation, the initial conditions for inflation itself, and the
   trans-Planckian problem. A fix that needs fixing.
10. **JWST early massive galaxies** [EMPIRICAL]. Galaxies too massive/mature too early for the
    CDM growth timeline.
11. **The dark-sector IOU** [meta]. 95% of the energy budget is two substances defined only by
    what they are not. *Why:* the model's successes are *conditional on* unverified components.

### 8.2 The honest hunt — can I find a paradox in SDT?

I tried to break it. I could not find a **[LOGICAL]** contradiction or a **[SELF-LIMITING]**
prediction-of-its-own-death. What I *did* find are **debts and tensions** — and intellectual
honesty (the same ruler from §0) requires naming them. None is a paradox; each is a place SDT is
exposed and must pay.

1. **The reproduction debt (the big one) — [DEBT, not paradox].** "No measurement problem" is
   purchased by an obligation: SDT must reproduce, *deterministically*, the phenomena QM gets
   right — double-slit interference, the Born statistics, and CHSH = 2√2. Bell's theorem means
   any such account must be **nonlocal**. SDT has the resource — the relay is "one tick from
   every boundary," a real nonlocal substrate with a preferred frame (the Bohmian escape hatch
   is open to it). But the *quantitative* reproduction is **not yet demonstrated**. Until it is,
   "SDT has no QM paradoxes" is a true statement about premises and a *promissory note* about
   results. This is where SDT is most exposed, and it should be said first, not buried.

2. **The neo-Lorentzian tension — [TENSION].** SDT's preferred frame must hide itself: no
   preferred-frame effect has been seen to ~10⁻¹⁸. Neo-Lorentzian relativity *can* do this
   (rods and clocks rescale with lattice motion via the movement budget, Law V), but it requires
   the substrate to conspire to be locally invisible, and it predicts an **absolute ceiling
   c_∞ (≈ c + 105 m/s)** that is, by its own logic, not *locally* measurable. A real quantity
   that cannot be directly observed is epistemically uncomfortable — the mirror-image of SR's
   one-way-speed conventionality (§1.5). Not a contradiction; a standing bill: exhibit a
   *second-order* preferred-frame observable (the depth-gradient anisotropy program) or concede
   the frame is forever indirect.

3. **The input/derivation circularity — [TENSION, and the closest thing to a paradox].**
   The permitted-inputs whitelist contains α and m_p, yet the framework claims to *derive* α
   (koppa at the hydrogen rung) and m_p/m_e (6π⁵). **You cannot both assume and derive the same
   number** without it being either a labelled consistency-check or a circularity. The audit
   spine exists precisely to catch this. The dependency graph must be made *acyclic*: fix the
   genuinely irreducible seed(s) — the programme's own note says *charge e is the one input*
   (CQ10/CQ37) and *ℓ_P needs one seed length* (CQ42 seed theorem) — and demote everything else
   to **derived** or **consistency-check**. Until that graph is published and acyclic, the
   "zero free parameters" banner is in tension with the 8-entry whitelist. This is the single
   place where SDT could *manufacture* a [LOGICAL] problem for itself if it got sloppy — and the
   discipline that prevents it (kill circular wins, label CALIBRATED honestly) is exactly the
   discipline already on display elsewhere in the repo.

4. **The no-attraction / stable-orbit obligation — [DEBT].** A push-only convergence field must
   yield *stable bound orbits* — closed ellipses, the right precession sign and magnitude — not
   merely the circular speed law v = c√(ϟ/r) (which already matches Kepler, and from which
   Mercury's precession has been argued geometrically). The full Kepler problem from pure
   pushing is partially shown, not finished. If pushing cannot make a stable ellipse, that
   *would* be a real problem; the burden is to complete the dynamics, not just the kinematics.

5. **The black-hole / strong-field obligation — [DEBT, narrowing].** SDT *does* have an event
   horizon (z=½, r=2ϟ, where orbital v=c/√2 and escape v=c — coinciding with the Schwarzschild

   ⅓ golden-spiral winding), throat zero-width/zero-matter. The obligation is to reproduce the
   EHT shadow, the ISCO, and the LIGO ringdown from *this* structure (horizon + koppa surface,
   no singularity). The ringdown investigation is now drafted (CQ44 line); the koppa-surface

### 8.3 Verdict

- **Paradoxes ([LOGICAL]/[SELF-LIMITING]) found in SDT: none.** Not by suppression — by
  construction. The premises that generate the others' paradoxes are not in SDT's axiom set.
- **Debts and tensions: five, named above.** The largest (the reproduction debt) is the real
  exposure; the sharpest (the input/derivation circularity) is the one SDT's own audit machinery

- The difference between SDT and the seven frameworks above is therefore *categorical*, not
  cosmetic: their open items include genuine contradictions and self-limiting forecasts; SDT's

  contradiction — it can only abandon the premise that caused it.** That is the whole argument,
