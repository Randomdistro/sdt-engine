> **Native definition of direction and duration (Harvey 2026-10-10).** A detector point adjacent to (or aligned with) moving material has a width much smaller than matter. Movement makes it contact an arriving edge of the material at location 1 and finish when the material leaves at location 2; the order of those two contacts is the direction, fixed by the movement itself. The duration is the count of cycles of an oscillator between the two contacts, and its rate against our own operation is what we call time (a quartz crystal counts its vibrations per second at a given voltage). ## 2. Analysis and extrapolation

### 2.1 What each class of rule guarantees (derived, then tested in §4)
1. **Doubly stochastic Markov rule** (R1 is symmetric, hence doubly stochastic): the Shannon entropy of the distribution over microstates cannot decrease, S(t+1) ≥ S(t) (concavity of −p ln p); it increases to ln W when the chain is ergodic. The "decrease" class is empty: it cannot be a test.
2. **General Markov rule with stationary distribution π** (detailed balance): the quantity that cannot increase is the relative entropy KL(p_t ‖ π) = Σ p ln(p/π). The Shannon entropy S itself is monotone only when π is uniform. A rule that favours some states (an aggregation bias) has a non-uniform π, and S can fall.
3. **Deterministic bijection on the microstates** (a reversible update): the fine-grained entropy of any ensemble is exactly constant (the map permutes probabilities). On a finite state space every orbit is periodic (Poincaré recurrence), with period at most W. A coarse-grained entropy of a single trajectory can rise only through the choice of macrostates; it returns to its start at the recurrence.
4. **Deterministic non-injective rule** (R3-type): the arrow is built into the rule, and the update stops at a fixed point that need not be the maximum-entropy macrostate.

### 2.2 Extrapolation of the lattice to physical scale (arithmetic, tested in §4 E4)
With the relay content per shell ε = u_CMB ℓ_P³ (Law I) and one cell per Planck volume, a volume V of the CMB holds E = uV/ε = V/ℓ_P³ quanta in N = V/ℓ_P³ cells: occupancy 1. Then:
- S_sb = k_B ln C(2N − 1, N) ≈ 2N ln 2 · k_B. For V = 1 m³: N = 2.4×10¹⁰⁴, so S_sb ≈ 3.3×10¹⁰⁴ k_B.
- The temperature from ∂S/∂E at occupancy 1: T_sb = ε/(k_B ln 2) ≈ 1.8×10⁻⁹⁵ K.
- The thermodynamic entropy density of the CMB, s = 4u/(3T) = 2.0×10⁻¹⁴ J/(K m³) = 1.5×10⁹ k_B per m³, and T = 2.7255 K.
So the Planck-cell stars-and-bars lattice with the stated ε overcounts the entropy by about 10⁹⁵ and undercounts the temperature by the same factor (S·T = E is the same in both). The microstate space that is thermodynamically accessible is therefore not "any distribution of ε-quanta over Planck cells". Requirement, not result: matching S_th with E = N needs N_eff = S_th/(2 ln 2 k_B) ≈ 1.1×10⁹ cells per m³, a cell of side about 1 mm; the standard thermal-photon count (about 3.6 k_B per photon, n_γ ≈ 4.1×10⁸ m⁻³) is the same number. This is a statement of what the accessible space must be, found by solving for the cell size from the measured entropy (a target-first inference), and is reported as such.

### 2.3 What the SDT premises imply for the arrow
- Movement has no sink and the relay is a deterministic pass ⇒ the micro-update is a bijection ⇒ the fine-grained entropy is conserved (2.1.3). The thermodynamic arrow then needs one of: a stated randomness in the relay (alignment search), an irreversible element, coarse-graining by observers made of the same medium, an aggregation bias (occlusion), or the initial condition.
- The occlusion push is an aggregation bias (bodies are pushed together). By 2.1.2 the stationary distribution of such a rule is non-uniform, so the occupancy entropy of an initially uniform state can fall (structure forms) while the relative entropy to the stationary measure still falls. A recompressed "static, even, hot, dense" initial state is then not the stationary state of the biased rule.

## 3. Hypotheses (native, registered before the experiments)
- **H-A (stochastic relay).** If the relay chooses among neighbours without bias, the exact Shannon entropy rises monotonically and relaxes to ln W in a time that scales as N² (diffusive) for a ring. Test E3.
- **H-B (observer coarse-graining of a reversible mixing relay).** A reversible, quantum-conserving, deterministic update that is not a swap produces a coarse-grained arrow: the macro-entropy of a single hot-start trajectory rises to ≥ 0.9 of its maximum and stays there on average, for every partition in a registered family, with recurrence at the exact orbit length. Test E1.
- **H-C (aggregation bias).** For a Metropolis-type exchange with stationary weight π(x) ∝ exp(β Σxᵢ²/E), β > 0, the Shannon entropy of the occupancy distribution started from the uniform distribution decreases to S_stat < ln W while KL(p_t ‖ π) decreases at every tick. Test E2.
- **H-D (accessible microstates).** The Planck-cell stars-and-bars lattice with ε = u ℓ_P³ does not reproduce the thermodynamic entropy or temperature of a radiation volume (overcount ≫ 1). Test E4.

### 8.2 What the expansion shows
1. **The entropy has three different meanings in the three rule classes**, and the "arrow" is a different statement in each: for a symmetric stochastic relay, the exact Shannon entropy rises to ln W with relaxation ∝ N² sweeps; for a reversible deterministic relay, the fine-grained entropy is constant and a coarse-grained arrow exists for a mixing rule (Q2) and not for regular rules (swap, Q1), lasting one orbit; for an aggregation-biased relay, the Shannon entropy of the occupancy can fall (structure forms: 8.9882 → 8.7018 at β = 0.5) while the relative entropy to the stationary measure falls monotonically.
2. **The reversible mixing arrow has no direction of its own.** A periodic orbit has the same rule forwards and backwards; the asymmetry is the low-entropy start (S/S_ref = 0 to 0.6 here). So for a deterministic relay the arrow in SDT inherits its direction from the initial condition, which the cyclic picture (Theory/07) must supply with an origin. This is a logical consequence of the permutation structure; the numerical backward check is open (O-1).
3. **Where the lattice does not match the thermodynamics.** With ε = uℓ_P³ and one cell per Planck volume, the stars-and-bars entropy and ∂S/∂E temperature of a CMB volume are 10⁹⁵ off in opposite directions; matching the measured entropy needs about 1.07×10⁹ cells per m³ (side 0.98 mm), which is the thermal photon count rescaled by 3.60/(2 ln 2) = 2.60 (B-006).
