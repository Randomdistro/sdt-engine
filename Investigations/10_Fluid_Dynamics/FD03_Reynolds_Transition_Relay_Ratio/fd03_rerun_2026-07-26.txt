----------------------------------------------------------------
 PHASE 1  Two lattice rates -> Re = UL/nu  (dimensionlessness)
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Advective transport (FD01 inertial term): a parcel's velocity
   field is carried downstream at speed U; over a scale L the
   transport time is
       tau_adv   = L / U          [s]   rate = U/L     [1/s]
 Relay smoothing (FD01/FD02 nu*grad^2 v): nearest-neighbour relay
   diffuses momentum; over scale L the smoothing time is
       tau_relay = L^2 / nu       [s]   rate = nu/L^2  [1/s]
 Both are TIMES of the SAME medium. Their ratio:
       Re = tau_relay / tau_adv = (L^2/nu)/(L/U) = U L / nu
 Dimensional proof (like-rate cancellation):
       [tau_relay]/[tau_adv] = [s]/[s] = 1   (DIMENSIONLESS)
       [U L / nu] = (m/s . m)/(m^2/s) = 1    (cross-check)

 Two-stream identity check (nu cancels; nu=MEASURED-INPUT):
   test point  U=0.1 m/s  L=0.02 m  nu=1e-06 m^2/s
   tau_adv    = 0.2 s     tau_relay = 400 s
   Re (ratio of times)  = 2000
   Re (U L / nu direct) = 2000
   relative difference  = 1.137e-16   [F1 PASS: identity exact]
   Law V budget: U/c = 3.336e-10 < 1 ; v_circ(U) = 299792458 m/s (<= c)

 PHASE 2  Coherence threshold  ->  Re_crit = (L/ell_c)^2
----------------------------------------------------------------
 Mechanism (DERIVED): laminar flow holds while relay smoothing
   damps the dominant disturbance mode over its coherence length
   ell_c BEFORE advection carries it across the channel scale L:
       tau_relay(ell_c) <= tau_adv(L)
   Smoothing acts coherently over ell_c:  tau_relay(ell_c)=ell_c^2/nu
   Advection carries over the full scale:  tau_adv(L)    = L/U
   The crossing tau_relay(ell_c)=tau_adv(L) gives the threshold:
       ell_c^2/nu = L/U   =>   U L / nu = (L/ell_c)^2
       Re_crit = (L / ell_c)^2 = G(geometry).
   Geometry enters ONLY through the coherence ratio (ell_c/L).
   nu CANCELS: Re_crit does not depend on the FD02 value (R5).
