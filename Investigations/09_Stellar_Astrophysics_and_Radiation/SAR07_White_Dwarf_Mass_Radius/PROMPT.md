# SAR07 — White Dwarf Mass–Radius from the Seat Stack

## Target

White dwarfs shrink as they gain mass — the inverse mass–radius relation — up to the 1.4 M☉
limit beyond which no white dwarf exists. Measured anchors: Sirius B (1.018 M☉, 0.0084 R☉)
and the Gaia white-dwarf sequence.

## SDT footing

The support is PRESS: movement that cannot move (L14). A full seat stack answers compression
by raising its top; the star balances its inward push against stack pressure. The inverse
relation and the mass limit should both fall out of exclusive-seat counting at stellar scale
— the same capped-occupancy machinery specified in the reopen register (R4) for metals, run
at a different density. The limit is where the stack top would demand budget the movement
ledger cannot supply (v_circ approaching c — Law V capping the stack). Existing corpus:
NP30 §11 (compression floor — the same physics one density regime up, which landed the
neutron-star 11.0 km), reopen R4 (the seat-stack counting run).

## Pre-registration skeleton (complete before any tool)

- Question: does exclusive-seat stack pressure against the occlusion push give R(M) with the

- Rejection criterion (populated): wrong trend sign, Sirius B off beyond tolerance, or a
  limit far from 1.4 M☉ (the counting could land anywhere — populated).
- Anchors: Sirius B; Gaia M–R sequence; the 1.4 M☉ ceiling.
