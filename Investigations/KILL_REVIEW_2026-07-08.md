## §H Adjudication 1 — SAR01 (the shakedown; the sink-correction case)

other), NOT absorption. FLOW: influx → body → the *blocked fraction's fate is the whole

**Phase 3 (label):** the over-general principle **mislabels the mode**. Le Sage specifically

overshoots ×2.3×10¹⁷ and is genuinely dead. But "absorption" writ large also covers BECOME
(matter banks movement into raised configuration / closed circulation) and PRESS (queue at a
refusal) — and those are **native**. The re-audit collapsed "no PASS-thermalisation sink at
this magnitude" into "no sink anywhere."
**Phase 5 (neighbour test — decisive):** if "absorption = movement-sink, forbidden" were held
as law, the neighbours SCREAM — atomic **excitation** (electron banks movement into raised
seat), orbital/spin **angular momentum** (closed circulation), and every **bond / raised mass
PE** (PRESS queue) become forbidden. All three are CANON/EARNED. The principle is refuted by

**Phase 6 (QM-contraband):** neither party leaned on a rival abstraction; the fault is an
internal over-generalisation of a mode, not an import.

**RULING (v1, WRONG — struck 2026-07-08 by Harvey's "by what mechanism?"):** claimed KILL
CONFIRMED + principle struck via the neighbour test. **RULING (v2, WRONG — same session):**
swung to KILL RESCOPED + storage route re-opened. Both fell to one category error:

**THE BUFFER / TERMINUS DISTINCTION (the actual correction).** The no-sink principle forbids a
**TERMINUS** — movement destroyed, no efflux — NOT a **BUFFER** — movement banked reversibly and
returned. Excitation (de-excites → emits), angular momentum (conserved circulation), and bond PE

it needs a terminus, and termini are forbidden (throughpole = no destruction). The re-audit's
