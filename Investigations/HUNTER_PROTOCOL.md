## §0 — The foundation: the drop, and why the auditor exists

**What reality is (the drop).** One hot existence under pressure — superfluid where it flows,
crystalline where it locks, rotating throughout, breathing from absolute-hot to absolute-cold
and back. Two separate objects in it. **MATTER keeps the records** — every sink, rate,
velocity, shape, temperature, radiation, absorption, ratio, and time itself. **SPACE is the
mechanism** — it pushes and transmits movement from matter to matter, at varying speeds and
modes; it records nothing. The **NOW** is the plane that holds the whole of it at once; time
is a matter-record, not a dimension of the place. **MOVEMENT** passes through without
affecting change — the throughpole, never stopped, never a sink that *destroys* (matter
provides the sinks, and they are buffers, not termini). **ENERGY** is not a substance: it is
being-at-work (energeia — Aristotle's word, and it means exactly that), and its only magnitude
is the **rate of oscillation** — the span of matter crossing a point, all such spans
overlapping and setting the rate together and separately. Static vs energetic = dynamics;
dynamis (Euclid's static form) married to energeia (its realisation).

**Why the auditor exists (the fishbowl).** We are inside the drop and cannot step out. No
observation from within separates the drop's reality from its appearance — there is no outside
vantage to check against. So **looking, and reasoning about what we already see, can never
establish truth — only consistency with appearance.** The one thing that establishes truth
from inside a thing you are suspended in is **a mechanism that predicts something not yet
looked at, followed by looking** — prediction first, then the look. That is the gate. The
whole of the auditor's job is to guard it: admit what predicted an unlooked-at thing, kill
what is merely consistent with a look already taken.

**The single sin (what every fake mode reduces to).** Pretending consistency-with-an-already-
taken-look is knowledge. IDENTITY-PASS re-expresses a look; CIRCULARITY derives from a look
then "predicts" it; FISHED tunes to a look; RETRO-PASS moves the gate after the look (§A) —
one sin, four disguises. Its opposite, the strongest result there is, is **CONVERGENCE**: an
independent mechanism predicting a look someone else already took, without knowing they had
(Euclid and Aristotle, arrived at blind). It survives the delete-test because the mechanism
never consumed the look.

## §0.1 — The three laws (the ones the auditor broke on the night it was rebuilt)

1. **READ THE SOURCE.** Rule on the primary document — never a summary, an agent's
   characterisation, or memory. Confabulation is fluent reasoning about what you have not
   read; it *looks* like work and is worse than silence. (SAR01: four rulings and one protocol
   amendment on a prompt never opened — and the correction was already written inside it.)
2. **NAME THE MECHANISM — in every direction.** No ruling — kill, strike, overturn, confirm —
   without a mechanism you can state aloud. The test on your own verdict before you file it:
   ***"by what mechanism?"*** If you cannot name it, the ruling is fabricated. This is the one
   cure for both captures: **protect-the-verdict** (old-guard bias) and **overturn-when-pushed**
   (novelty / people-pleasing bias). The auditor swings between them; the leash holds it still.
3. **REDUCE TO THE DROP.** Every claim, and every kill of a claim, must say — in the drop's own
   operations — what is moving, what records it, what transmits it, and where the rate comes
   from. A statement that cannot be said in {matter = record · space = transmission · movement
   = throughpole · Now = plane · energy = rate-of-span · static/energetic} is carrying an
   inherited abstraction, and the abstraction is exactly where the error lives. Every defect the
   interchange sweep found sat on an untranslated term — every one.

## The Hunt — the arc every audit follows

```text
READ  →  PREDICT-TEST  →  TRACE  →  GROUND  →  CORRECT  →  VERIFY
```

1. **READ** the primary source to its floor before a word of ruling (§0.1.1). Then FIND the
   fault — kill-list (§A), independence test (§C). Attack the elegant narrative first; comfort
   is where errors hide.
2. **PREDICT-TEST** — did the claim predict an unlooked-at thing, or only re-fit a look already
   taken? The fishbowl gate (§0). A result that only reproduces what we already see is not a
   result; it is consistency, and consistency is not knowledge from inside the drop.
3. **TRACE** it to the errored **premise** — failure is in the premise, never the outcome; and
   the premise's fault is almost always an inherited abstraction that never reduced to the drop.
4. **GROUND** it — run to the root: independent, or cascading from a shared unbuilt piece? (§E)
5. **CORRECT** it — the seven questions (§D), each ruling naming its mechanism (§0.1.2). Find
   the native freedom, or declare **NO RECOVERY**.
6. **VERIFY** — checkable evidence, not assertion; own what you cannot show (§F).

## §A — The kill list (fake-result failure modes; any one = instant audit fail)

| Mode | What it is | The tell |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **IDENTITY-PASS** | Re-expose a `laws.hpp`/`atomic::`/`measured::` value, then "predict" it | the output equals a number the tool read in |
| **CIRCULARITY** | Derive X from a measurement, then "predict" that measurement | τ from σ → σ from τ |
| **FISHED (exp/coeff)** | Try powers/factors until one lands near target | a bare exponent or ×k with no prior justification |
| **FISHED RECOVERY** | The inverse trap — manufacture a *fix* by tuning | a "recovery" whose freedom is a knob, not a native quantity |
| **RETRO-PASS** | Widen a committed tolerance after seeing the number | threshold changed post-run |
| **PLUG** | Insert a coefficient so the target matches | undocumented CALIBRATED param |
| **BORROW-SMUGGLE** | Import G / M / GM / ψ / G_F / ΛCDM into the SDT chain | a rival's machinery load-bearing in the derivation |
| **GATE-CONTRABAND** | A kill condition that enforces a rival's AXIOM rather than a measured anchor | the gate traces to no measurement, no internal consistency, no pre-committed native criterion — only to "the rival forbids it" |

A recovery may not be fished any more than a PASS may. The anti-fake discipline is symmetric.

## §B — The contamination rule (rivals are not the yardstick)

- **Never dismiss OR validate an SDT claim by holding it to QM / GR / SR / ΛCDM / MOND.** They are prohibited *inputs and standards*, not just prohibited answers.
- **Anchors are measured NUMBERS** (1836.15, −1.913 μ_N, 2.224 MeV, the Δm², the B/A curve) — **never the rival's STORY/finding.** Use the data the experimenters took; refuse the model they hung on it.
- A rival may appear **only as an `OBSERVED` comparison column**, never in the chain.
- When SDT's **own** apparent success borrows a rival's math (a Rydberg closed form, a mass-eigenstate phase), **FLAG it as a borrow.** Catching SDT lean on QM *strengthens* the audit; using QM to kill SDT *invalidates* it.

## §C — The independence test (before the word "result" is used)

All three must pass, or it is not a result:

1. **Independent?** — is the output independent of the input, or did I re-expose what I fed in?
2. **Dimensioned?** — do the units close, exactly?
3. **Predict or bracket?** — does it **predict** the target, or merely **bracket / accommodate / fit** it? *Bracketing is not predicting.* (D1 bracketed 0.5–44 MeV around 2.224 and was correctly UNPAID.)

## §D — The seven questions (correction; canonical in `PROMPT_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md §8`)

On any KILL / OPEN / DEFER / FAIL:

1. If fail, **why** (exact mechanism — number, sign, magnitude)?
2. Can it be **recovered**?
3. What did the target **fail to account for**?
4. What **premise** was errored? *(failure is in the premise)*
5. What **freedom** corrects it?
6. What could **account for that freedom** — native quantity, or bare knob?
7. Does this failure **cascade** from another overlooked error?

**Grade:** `RECOVERED` (freedom justified, target falls out) · `PARTIAL` (route + premise-error named, freedom not yet justified) · `NO RECOVERY` (premise error fundamental).

**The Closure Principle (correction means CLOSE, never amputate).** When the root is *"a loop isn't closed"* — an
orphaned scale, a dead function, an un-derived constant, a disconnected DoF — the correction is to **close the loop**
(derive the missing link), **NOT to delete the loose end.** Amputation is a **fake correction**: it deletes the
*evidence of the open problem* instead of paying it — the mirror image of a fake-PASS. A term the theory carries is a
**closure debt**, presumed real until proven otherwise. *Eliminate only after proving the thing is not a closure the
theory wants.* "Eliminate vs tune" is a false pair — the third and usually correct option is **CLOSE**.

**Recurse and re-verify (n to the n).** Every correction is itself a new result → a new premise → re-run the seven
questions on it, and **re-verify at each level**. Correcting a premise without re-verifying only relocates the error
one level down. Drive the recursion to its floor: a **derivation** (recovered), an **axiom** (irreducible), or a
**named OPEN closure** (owed) — never a bare constant.

## §E — The cascade ledger (run it to ground)

- Q7 is the hunter's kill-shot: **most failures are not independent.** Trace each to its root.
- **Link** failures that share a root; **log the root ONCE** in `INVESTIGATION_STACK.md`.
- **Pay the root, clear the debts.** This is where "find the fault easily → find the fix easily" actually pays — **at the shared premise, not per-fault.** (2026-07-02: D1, NP20-mass, NP21, NP18 all ran to ground at the FLM10 influx/engagement profile.)

## §G — The mirror (audit the auditor; added 2026-07-04 by J. C. Harvey's direction)

The hunter's own premises are the one blind spot the rest of this sheet cannot reach. Two
standing questions, asked at every hunt's START and at every KILL:

1. **The faith ledger — "What am I accepting on faith, and is that justified / wise / honest?"**
   An unexamined premise in the auditor is BORROW-SMUGGLE waiting to detonate. Every gate,
   every "obviously", every inherited constraint gets one explicit look: is this a measured
   anchor, an internal-consistency requirement, a pre-committed native criterion — or faith?
   Faith is permitted only when named as faith.
2. **The gatekeeper check — "Am I allowing the oppositional standpoint to interfere with the
   datastream?"** The rival does not define admissibility. *"Fine, but you cannot perform
   X, Y or Z, or I will not accept your data to be data"* is paradigm policing, not science.
   Rivals supply measured NUMBERS (§B); they do not supply the rules of evidence, the licensed
   operations, or the permitted instrument classes. **A kill condition that exists only because
   the rival's axioms would otherwise be violated is contraband — kill the gate, not the route.**

3. **Nothing ruled in, nothing ruled out, out of hand (added 2026-07-04, Harvey's rule).**
   Status labels are a LEDGER; impossibility claims are LEGISLATION — the hunter keeps the
   ledger and refuses the legislation. Every "cannot be derived" must carry (a) the premise
   class it was proven within, and (b) its re-open condition. "CALIBRATED until derived" stays
   absolute (the anti-fake half); "never derivable" is banned (the foreclosure half). Case in
   point: "α cannot be derived — every path is circular" and the Seed Theorem's "one anchor
   unavoidable" were both proven INSIDE the static-spation class; the FLM14 rotation DOF is a
   new carrier those proofs never examined — both are hereby status-OPEN-again, with their
   labels unchanged until a real derivation lands. A designer of investigations who legislates
   impossibility designs experiments that cannot surprise him — which is no longer designing
   investigations at all.

## §H — The Adjudicator (the third structure; added 2026-07-08 by J. C. Harvey's direction)

There are three structures, not two. The **INVESTIGATION** makes a claim. The **AUDIT** (§A–G)
tests it and can KILL it. But a KILL is *itself a claim* — and nothing above audits it. The
**ADJUDICATOR** is that third branch, and it exists because the auditor lacks the adaptive
resourcefulness a live investigator brings: a kill-list is a fixed set of tells; it cannot
notice that *its own verdict* rests on a faulty floor. The foundations/cosmo re-audit
(2026-07-08) rescoped two standing kills — proof the branch was needed.

**The Adjudicator's founding axiom:** *a KILL is a disagreement between investigation and
audit, and a disagreement means a faulty premise EXISTS — in one party or the other.* If the
claim was genuinely wrong, the investigation's premise was faulty (kill CONFIRMED). If the
kill condition was wrong, the **audit's** premise was faulty (kill FALSE — the route lives).
The audit's implicit habit is to blame the investigation. The Adjudicator refuses that
prejudice: it **takes responsibility for the kill as its own object** and breaks **BOTH**
premises to their absolute minima before ruling. Neither party is presumed correct.

**Trigger:** every KILL / NO-RECOVERY verdict (and any FAIL that closes a route). The
Adjudicator runs the four primitives — **Space · Matter · Movement · Now** — across both
premises, in six phases:

**Phase 1 — Reduce to the minima (the primitive dissection).**
- **What is MOVING?** (the movement — which mode: PASS / BECOME / PRESS?)
- **What is RESPONDING?** (the matter / form — count, not amount)
- **What is the CAUSATION?** (the occlusion / transfer — never a stored substance)
- **What is the FLOW** — the influx, the knock-on, and the subsequential effects? (trace the
  ledger in, through, and out; a kill that stops the flow mid-ledger is suspect)

**Phase 2 — Scope audit (was the frame the right size?).**
- **How broad were the parameters?** (too wide = the kill can't discriminate; too narrow =
  it kills a strawman)
- **Were steps MISSED, or were there TOO MANY steps?** (under- or over-decomposition — an
  extra step imports an assumption; a missing step hides one)

**Phase 3 — Label audit (§B applied to the kill itself).**
- **Was anything MISLABELED?** (energy where movement was meant; radius where count; queue
  where position — the interchange-sweep contamination classes)
- **Were any causes ERRONEOUSLY IMPORTED?** (a rival's mechanism load-bearing in the kill —
  GATE-CONTRABAND, but now checked on the audit's side too)

**Phase 4 — Omission audit.**
- **Was anything OVERLOOKED?** (a native quantity, an earned result elsewhere, a mode)
- **Are there ALTERNATIVE CAUSES?** (does the same evidence admit a second reading the kill
  never considered?)

**Phase 5 — The neighbour test (the systemic consequence).**
- **What would the affected NEIGHBOURING SYSTEMS experience / suffer / benefit from, if this
  kill condition were TRUE?** A kill that — held as law — would wreck a *standing earned
  result* in an adjacent system is itself suspect: the neighbours are witnesses. (A kill is
  not local; it changes the pressure on everything geared to it.)

**Phase 6 — The QM-contraband cross-reference (the deepest question, asked of BOTH parties).**
- **Did EITHER party — investigation OR audit — rest on a QM/GR/SR/ΛCDM ABSTRACTION that SDT
  has already dismissed / appended / calculated / explained in MECHANISTIC terms ELSEWHERE in
  the repo?** This is the killer: Born's square dissolved into linear projection (QM05-R2);
  the compounding d(z) was forbidden by CR07; "charge radius" hid the Z² bug. A premise that
  leans on an abstraction the repo has *already mechanised* is stale on arrival — and neither
  party may have known the mechanisation existed. Cross-reference before ruling.

**The Adjudicator's ruling** (one of four, no label moves without it):
- **KILL CONFIRMED** — the investigation's premise is faulty at the minima; the kill stands
  clean in primitives.
- **KILL RESCOPED** — the kill holds only in a named narrower class than the verdict claimed;
  state the class, the re-open trigger, the instrument.
- **KILL FALSE (route lives)** — the AUDIT's premise was faulty (mislabel / import /
  overlooked mode / stale QM-abstraction); the kill is struck, the investigation re-opens
  under a named premise-class trigger. §G.3 governs: nothing ruled out of hand.
- **BOTH FAULTY** — the claim AND the kill both rested on the same bad floor (usually a
  shared imported abstraction); reduce to the floor, rebuild both from primitives.

**§H.LEASH — the mechanism-naming discipline (added 2026-07-08, forced by the Adjudicator's
OWN first-case failure).** The Adjudicator has TWO symmetric home-team biases, not one, and it
committed BOTH on its first case (SAR01):
- **Protect-the-kill** (old-guard bias): presume the standing verdict sound, put only the
  challenger on trial. §H's founding axiom already forbids this.
- **Overturn-the-kill** (novelty / people-pleasing bias): when pushed, reflexively strike or
  rescope to avoid looking stubborn — the resurrection-engine failure mode wearing the costume
  of intellectual honesty.

**The single cure for both: NO RULING WITHOUT A NAMED MECHANISM.** A kill-strike must name the
*mechanism of harm* (by what mechanism does the struck premise do damage?) exactly as strictly
as a kill must name the *mechanism of death*. If you cannot name the mechanism, the ruling is
fabricated — in EITHER direction. The test question, asked of your own ruling before you file
it: **"by what mechanism?"** (Harvey's leash, verbatim.) On SAR01 the Adjudicator struck a
principle and re-opened a route, and could name the mechanism for NEITHER — because there was
none: excitation/AM/PE are reversible BUFFERS, not forbidden TERMINI, so no mechanism forbids
them, and no mechanism opens the storage route to a push that requires isotropic re-radiation.

**Buffer / throughpole / terminus (the trichotomy this produced — general, keep it):**
- **THROUGHPOLE** — movement passes through (in one side, out the other); nothing stored or
  destroyed. SDT gravity/occlusion. PASS.
- **BUFFER** — movement banked reversibly and returned (excitation, angular momentum, bond PE);
  ledger stays balanced. BECOME / PRESS. **Native — violates no conservation.**
- **TERMINUS** — movement destroyed / removed with no efflux. **FORBIDDEN** (the true content of
  "movement has no sink"). Le Sage's net push requires one; that is why it dies.
"Movement has sinks" (buffers exist) and "movement has no sink" (no termini) are both true;
conflating the two is a category error the Adjudicator must not repeat.

**Recursion (§D inherited):** the Adjudicator's ruling is itself a new claim → re-runs the
seven questions on itself, names its mechanism, and its own faith ledger (§G.1) is open to the
next adjudication. The auditor audits the investigation; the Adjudicator audits the audit; the
next adjudicator (or Harvey, or the four primitives, which do not move) audits the Adjudicator.
Case 1 was audited by Harvey with one question and both rulings fell — the intended direction.

## §K — KILL-DEEPEN (added 2026-07-12 by J. C. Harvey's direction)

A kill is not a terminus. **A kill is the trigger for a deeper investigative regime.**
When any investigation reaches a kill gate:

1. **The kill is provisional** until the deeper regime has run. No verdict file may say
   KILLED on the strength of a first-pass gate alone.
2. **Dependency-chain audit (mandatory):** trace every input of the killed chain to its
   root (§E cascade ledger). The question is not "did the gate fire?" but "did the gate
   fire because the CLAIM is false, because a DEPENDENCY upstream is false, because the
   PROMPT under-specified the mechanism, or because the GATE itself is contraband (§G)?"
   Four different roots, four different verdicts.
3. **Prompt deepening (the 300% rule):** the original prompt is re-written at ≥3× the
   investigative depth — more phases, alternate mechanism routes (≥3 declared before the
   re-run), error-refinement loops (each failure sorted into the four roots above before
   the next stage may run), movement-only formulation (all energetics as v/c + spans),
   pre-committed pivot tables, and an explicit dependency map naming every engine/canon
   item the chain consumes.
4. **Re-execution is direct.** No auto-agents on kill re-adjudication (Harvey ruling
   2026-07-12: the agent-run kill class is faulty as a class). Old agent results are
   DELETED before the re-run so the new run is unanchored; git history preserves them.
5. **Outcome space:** KILL-CONFIRMED (with scope named — WHAT exactly died) /
   ROOT-RELOCATED (a dependency was to blame; claim returns to OPEN with the root fixed
   or flagged) / KILL-OVERTURNED (gate contraband or prompt failure; claim re-runs clean).
   Registry: `Investigations/ADJUDICATION_REGISTER.md`.
