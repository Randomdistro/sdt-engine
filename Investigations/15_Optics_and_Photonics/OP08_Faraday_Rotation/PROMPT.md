# OP08 — Faraday Rotation: Handed Speeds in a Turned Medium

> **Status:** OPEN — specification seed (benchmark-coverage alignment 2026-07-29; catalogue
> row B97). **Author:** J. C. Harvey, Melbourne. Gateways binding.

## Target

Polarised light passing through a medium along an applied magnetic field rotates its
polarisation plane by β = V·B·d — the Verdet linearity — with V characteristic of the
medium. Measured anchors: Verdet constants for standard glasses and water.

## SDT footing

An applied field is imposed collective circulation (PM01); light is transverse relay (OP04:
polarisation as wake orientation). In a circulating medium the two handed transverse
components relay at slightly different speeds — the medium's turning adds to one hand and
subtracts from the other — so the plane rotates linearly with path and with the imposed
circulation. The Verdet constant should reduce to the medium's gradient-index response
(OP01) times the circulation coupling. Existing corpus: OP01, OP04, PM01, FD05 (the
one-criterion family for medium-speed effects).

## Pre-registration skeleton (complete before any tool)

- Question: does handed-speed splitting in an imposed circulation give β = V·B·d with V
  computed from the medium's measured index dispersion, no per-medium fit?
- Rejection criterion (populated): computed V off beyond tolerance for two independent
  media, or nonlinearity where measurement shows Verdet linearity.
- Anchors: Verdet constants, ≥2 media.
- Tolerance: committed before instrumentation.
