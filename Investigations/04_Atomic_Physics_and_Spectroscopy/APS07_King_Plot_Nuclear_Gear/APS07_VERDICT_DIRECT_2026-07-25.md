## What is settled (arithmetic, nothing else — corrected per Harvey 2026-07-25)

Triton count is a **structural count** — the number of n-p-n clusters the grammar builds with:
`n_t = A − 2Z`. It is not a function of A across the chart (Ca-48 carries n_t = 8; Ti-48 carries
n_t = 4, same A). The neutron ledger is **N = 2 + n_d + 2·n_t** (two in the alpha, one per
deuteron, **two per triton**). Between adjacent isotopes at fixed Z the two decompositions differ
by (δn_d, δn_t) = (−1, +1): one triton more per excess neutron, and the triton-held neutron
count moves by two. That is a difference between **two nuclei's ledgers — no process is claimed**;
each nucleus is its own construction with one way to be. Where the ledgers come from (Harvey,
2026-07-26): the deuteron and the triton are forged as their own objects in stellar fusion, and
heavy-element assembly runs **behind the stellar c boundary** — neighbouring isotopes are
different castings from the same brick stock, not stages of one another. **The triton ledger
moves at 2n; only the count's ORDER coincides with N.** A King plot holds one element, so Z is frozen
and n_t is monotone in N along the chain. Consequence — and it is the only consequence: a bare
re-SORT of one element's residuals by n_t reproduces the N-sort. **The sort, and only the sort,
is struck as an evidence move.**
