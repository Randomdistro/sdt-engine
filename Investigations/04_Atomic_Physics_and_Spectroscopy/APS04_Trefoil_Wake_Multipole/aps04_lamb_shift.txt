────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ORBITAL GEOMETRY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Electron orbits in the trefoil wake at r = a₀ (hydrogen ground state):

EQUATORIAL ORBIT ("s-like"):
  - Electron in the trefoil's equatorial plane (θ = π/2)
  - Passes through all three lobes of the trefoil per revolution
  - Sees maximal quadrupole interaction: cos(3φ) averaged = +1/3
  - Energy: E_eq = E_0 - (3/2)Φ₂(a₀) + δE_trefoil

POLAR ORBIT ("p-like"):
  - Electron perpendicular to the trefoil axis (θ = 0 or π)
  - Stays near the proton's axis, averages out the three-lobe structure
  - Sees minimal quadrupole interaction: cos(3φ) averaged ≈ 0
  - Energy: E_pol = E_0 + 0 + δE_small

ENERGY DIFFERENCE:
  ΔE = E_eq - E_pol ≈ -(3/2)Φ₂(a₀)

================================================================================

MEASURED VALUE:

                = 4.4013 × 10^{-7} eV
                = 7.0533 × 10^{-26} J

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ENERGY SCALING FROM MULTIPOLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The quadrupole energy splitting scales as:

  ΔE_2 = (3/2) × Φ₂(a₀) × |P₂(cos π/2) - P₂(cos 0)|
       = (3/2) × Φ₂(a₀) × |-1/2 - 1|
       = (3/2) × Φ₂(a₀) × 3/2
       = (9/4) × Φ₂(a₀)

The trefoil contribution:

  ΔE_3 = Φ₃(a₀) × [cos(3φ)_eq - cos(3φ)_pol]
       ≈ Φ₃(a₀) × 1/3

TOTAL FINE STRUCTURE SPLITTING:

  ΔE_fine = α² Ry × [1 + (9/4)(Φ₂/r_e) + (1/3)(Φ₃/r_e) + ...]
          = α² × 13.606 eV × [1 + correction_multipole]

where Ry = 13.605693 eV is the Rydberg constant.
