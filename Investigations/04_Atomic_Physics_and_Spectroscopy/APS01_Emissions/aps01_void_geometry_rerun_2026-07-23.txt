  VERDICT: TRITON PILEUP AND SHELL OPENINGS

  The neutron excess n_t = A - 2Z grows FASTEST at d/f boundaries.
  s/p block (Z=1-20):  n_t stays 0-1 (deuteron dominated)
  3d opens (Z=21):     n_t JUMPS to 3+ and climbs through d-block
  3d closes (Z=30):    element returns to DIAMAGNETIC
  Pattern repeats at 4d (Z=39-48) and 5d (Z=72-80).
  f-block (Z=58-70):   n_t climbs STEEPLY (lanthanides)
  f-block (Z=90+):     n_t climbs even faster (actinides)

  The nuclear grammar REQUIRES more tritons precisely where
  the electronic structure opens d and f shells.
  The tritons ARE the d/f slots.
