## SDT footing

Charge is handedness (L12): matter and antimatter are the two winding hands. A universe with
a net hand means the detonation's release was not hand-symmetric, or the medium's
frustration-herded voids (L1) price one hand's knots infinitesimally differently. The kaon
system — where the two hands mix and beat — is the laboratory-scale probe of that pricing
difference. Existing corpus: EMC03 (repulsion/handedness), PPT09, NP33 §1 (the mesh is
built on hand-alternation — a hand-pricing asymmetry would propagate into it).
