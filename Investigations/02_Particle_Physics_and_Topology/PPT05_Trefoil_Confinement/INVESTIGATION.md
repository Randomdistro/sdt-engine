## Result

For a throughput tube whose cross-section is held fixed by the registered boundary condition, its volume and energy are linear in separation:

\[
E(L)=\sigma L.
\]

The instrument uses the trefoil geometry

\[
R=\frac{\hbar}{m_pc\sqrt{3}},\qquad
\rho=\frac{m_p}{2\pi^2Ra^2},\qquad A=\pi a^2,
\]

so

\[
\sigma=\rho c^2A=\frac{m_pc^2}{2\pi R}=1.230\ {\rm GeV/fm}.
\]

The coefficient is computed from the mass/action seat and registered trefoil geometry. Its 37% difference from the cited `0.9 GeV/fm` comparison is a residual, not a fitted agreement.
