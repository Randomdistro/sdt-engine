# Coincidences addendum (opened 2026-10-07, at Harvey's direction)

**Purpose.** A register of serious coincidences, in the actual meaning of the term: incidences that co-exist, converge, or otherwise match in interesting or unforeseen manners. They are recorded without interpretation and are not evidence for or against any mechanism. They are held for later consideration, or used at once where an investigation finds one pertinent.

**What does not belong here.** A near-match found by scanning candidate forms against a target (a census); a relation true by construction (an arithmetic rearrangement of the same measured numbers). Such items may be listed in the "constructions and census items" section, so that they are not mistaken for coincidences later.

**Entry fields.** id · date · found in · statement (with the numbers and their source file) · kind (co-existing / converging / matching) · status (unexplained / explained by construction / explained by a named mechanism) · look-elsewhere (how many alternatives were available when it was noticed; whether it was noticed after the target was known) · what would make it serious (the independent observable or derivation) · pertinent now (yes/no, where).
