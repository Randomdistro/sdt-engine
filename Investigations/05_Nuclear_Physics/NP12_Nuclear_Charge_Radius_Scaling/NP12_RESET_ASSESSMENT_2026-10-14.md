If V2 fails, the definition of the chain growth differs from the prompt's and that is reported before anything else is read.
**Quantities.** (i) Per chain: G_meas, G_null, G_H2(η_d) for η_d on a grid (0.40…0.74), by the endpoint ratio R(N_max)/R(N_min) from H2 with the same endpoints; (ii) the median over chains of the per-chain growth error |G_H2 − G_meas|/G_meas as a function of η_d, and the median ratio G_H2/G_meas; (iii) the η_d at which the median ratio equals 1 (bisection), the required value; (iv) the same at the bracket ends 0.473 and 0.561 and at η_d = η_cp (H2 reduces to the baseline, chain growth from A^{1/3} only plus the n-dependence of f_t cancelling, which gives the null); (v) the shape residual of the linear form: per-chain correlation of the model and measured growth at the required η_d.
**Decisions.** Gate B3 (median per-chain growth error ≤ 3%) is not evaluable without a derived η_d (D1); it is applied only at the NP30 bracket ends, as information, with the explicit statement that no η_d is claimed. The required η_d is a **target for the owed derivation**. Inside the NP30 bracket ⇒ "consistent with the mechanism's own range"; outside ⇒ "inconsistent: the linear form or the bracket is wrong".
**Cascade bands.** G2 (open: the one element is undetermined; the required η_d reported); G4 only if a derived η_d lands within 3% of the median slope (not available).
**Expected landing (hand estimate before registering).** V0 908; V1 4.96 ± 0.3; V2 passes if the chain definition is the endpoint growth (the prompt's 2.36 and 5.10 are averages over ΔN ≈ 15). The required η_d lies between the bracket ends because the measured slope is about half the A^{1/3} rate: half the mass growth must be compensated by f_t rising along a chain, i.e. a modest offset (η_cp − η_d ≈ 0.2 per unit f_t over a Δf_t of order 0.1…0.15 per 15 neutrons): η_d ≈ 0.4–0.55, near the bracket.
