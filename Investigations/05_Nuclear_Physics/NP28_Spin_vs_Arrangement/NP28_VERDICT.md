# NP28 — Nuclear Magnetic Moments: Intrinsic Spin vs Arrangement

> **Question (Harvey):** is "spin" an intrinsic property, or bookkeeping over
> circulation + arrangement? Test: does the pure-intrinsic-spin model (Schmidt)
> predict measured nuclear moments? Run 2026-07-19, Fable direct.
> Data: textbook/Stone moments + firm shell assignments [VERIFY before certification].

## Result

Schmidt = moment attributed to ONE nucleon's INTRINSIC SPIN (free-nucleon g_s).
Confronted with 26 measured moments:

| arrangement | mean deviation from Schmidt |
|---|---|
| genuine single-particle (doubly-magic ±1 nucleon) | **few %** |
| mid-shell / collective | **30–70%** |

The clean single-particle cases sit ON the intrinsic-spin prediction:
O-17 (−1%), H-1 (0%), F-19 (−6%), Pb-207 (−7%), C-13 (+10%), He-3 (+11%).
The collective cases quench massively toward the interior: P-31 −59%, I-127 −51%,
La-139 −52%, Si-29 −71%, Fe-57 −86%.

**Honesty fix to the raw run:** percentage deviations for near-zero Schmidt values
(K-39 +215%, Mn-55 +300%, Ti-47 −158%) are small-denominator artifacts — their
ABSOLUTE deviations (≈0.3–2.6 μN) are ordinary. The robust pattern uses absolute μN
and is unchanged: single-particle → on the line; collective → quenched.

## What this establishes (and what it does not)

**AGAINST intrinsic-spin sufficiency — WON:** the moment is predicted by a fixed
intrinsic spin ONLY when the arrangement is genuinely one nucleon. When the
arrangement is collective, intrinsic-spin fails by 30–70%. Therefore **the moment
tracks the ARRANGEMENT, not a fixed intrinsic spin.** The standard rescue (config
mixing + meson-exchange currents + g_s quenched to ~0.7 g_free) IS the admission
that the moment is the whole configuration's circulation. Consistent with Harvey:
"spin" = bookkeeping label over (handed circulation + position). The single-sub-unit
circulation strength (g_s) is real and mechanical; calling its collective sum
"intrinsic spin" is the label laid on top.

**FOR the SDT positional number — NOT YET (§H.LEASH):** this run defeats the rival's
spin; it does NOT build the arrangement's winning number. SDT must produce the
quenching from ACTUAL sub-unit circulation cancellation at real positions — a number,
not a mechanism sketch. Prior magnetism pass (NP24-A) came back DEGENERATE
(count-parity). So: door open, not walked through.

**The buildable lead:** quenching = positional PARTIAL CANCELLATION of handed sub-unit
circulations. Schmidt has NO natural quenching (needs ad-hoc g_s^eff ≈ 0.7 g_free);
the arrangement gives cancellation for free — but must reproduce the per-nucleus
quench from geometry to convert this from "consistent with" to "predicted by".
That is NP28's owed next step and the place spin-as-bookkeeping becomes a number.
