# NSEQ19 — contact-closure stacker: results (runs of 2026-10-15/16; registration `NSEQ19_REGISTRATION_2026-10-15.md`)

Instrument: `nseq19_stack.cpp` (MSVC build `build.bat`), module orders and seat directions from `nseq19_export.js` (the live engine's `universalShellAllocation`; engine read only). Units of R_p; hard spheres of radius 1; contact = unlike pair with centre distance < 2.01; no overlap; no like contact (like pairs ≥ 2.01); at most 3 contacts per unit; connected. All 296 nuclides with A ≥ 4 in the set (252 stable + 32 primordial + the T8/T10 series members) were run; A < 4 are trivial (a single module).

## G1 — does every nucleus pack?
- **Registered settings** (seat cone 90°, 24 restarts per module, 3 seeds): **250 of 296** pack. The 46 failures all stop at a module of an early shell ("no valid contact pose for module 4–10 of D6/D12-pre" in most; 2 disconnected). 46 of 296 = 15.5 %, under the registered catastrophe line of 25 %.
- **Retry of those 46** (seat cone removed, 48 restarts, 10 seeds; registered as the sensitivity): **46 of 46 pack.** So all 296 pack. The 46 are packed without the seat-cone constraint (their modules may sit more than 90° from the registered seat direction; how far was not measured).
- **V2 independent checker passed for every reported state** (separate brute-force routine: no overlap, no like contact, degree ≤ 3, connected); max degree 3 everywhere; mean contacts per unit 2.3 (median); all connected.
- V1 known answers: ⁴He alone gives 4 contacts, every degree 2; ⁶Li, ⁷Li, ⁹Be (alpha plus one or two modules) pack. V3 (energy never rises within a relaxation) is enforced by the acceptance rule, not logged separately.
- **Size, a result I did not tune:** the extent of the packed nuclei is close to the close-packed value R(A) = R_p (A/η)^(1/3): over the first run E_ext / (close-pack extent) has median 1.195 (range 0.876–1.315); in the five-seed series the rms radius is 1.04–1.08 × the close-packed rms radius (0.775 (A/η)^(1/3)). For A = 40 the engine's live stacking gave an extent of 12.4 R_p; this stacker gives 5.07 ± 0.05 (close-packed 4.78).

## G2 — Harvey's triton-shell rule (five independent seeds per nuclide, uniform settings: no cone, 48 restarts, 10 seeds each; `nseq19_series_analysis.txt`)
- **T8 opening (n_t = 0 → 1) expands:** E_ext 5.066 ± 0.049 → 5.533 ± 0.164 (+0.47, about 2.7 pooled σ); the rms radius rises 3.042 → 3.164. Extent relative to close-packed rises 1.060 → 1.136 and peaks at 1.220 (n_t = 3).
- **T8 closure (n_t = 8) is not back and not tighter:** E_ext 6.233 ± 0.223, +1.17 above n_t = 0 (absolute; the registered reading), ratio to close-packed 1.150 against 1.060; the rms ratio rises monotonically 1.038 → 1.071. The extent ratio does fall from its peak (1.220 at n_t = 3 to 1.125 at n_t = 7) before closure, but closure itself adds nothing.
- **T10:** E_ext at n_t = 0 → 1 is unchanged (5.978 → 5.979); n_t = 8 → 9 is +0.09 (below 1 σ); the extent then rises to 7.4–7.6 and the closure at n_t = 18 (7.504) is +0.875 above the T8-closed state (6.629). Extent relative to close-packed: 1.103 (n_t = 0), 1.120 (T8 closed), 1.169 (T10 closed).
- **Is the closure relaxation too weak?** Repeating the T8 series with ten times the closure moves (3000 N, three seeds) changed E_ext by −0.19 to +0.25 per nuclide with no trend and the rms by < 0.05: the downhill rigid-body relaxation is already jammed. Post-seen diagnostic, not a registered gate.
- **Verdict on G2 as registered:** opening expands for T8 and not for T10; closing is not back or tighter for either. **Not met.** Not catastrophic by the registered definition (G1 met after the sensitivity; opening expansion present in one of two series; checker intact).

## What this says
- Contact-only stacking, with the three-slot rule and hard rim-sized envelopes, produces compact, connected structures for every nucleus in the set at about the close-packed size. That removes the engine's sprawl as a feature of the contact rule.
- Harvey's open/close behaviour does **not** come out of pure downhill collapse of rigid modules. In this model the contacts that form at a shell closure add nothing that pulls the structure in. If closure tightening is a real effect of the connections formed, the model needs those connections to *do* something: an attraction that grows with the number of contacts (a contact-lowered energy in place of the pure centrality energy Σ r²), or non-rigid modules (units that rotate or slide within a module), neither of which is in this instrument. Neither has been registered or run.
- Limits (from the registration): rigid modules; spheres of radius R_p (NP30 envelope), not filament bundles; static geometry, no flow, no meshing phase, no internal electron; random search, so "packs" means a valid state was found, not that it is unique or lowest; the 46 first-pass failures needed the seat cone removed.

## Corrections and post-seen controls (2026-10-16, after an adversarial re-read)
- **Significance misquoted above.** "+0.47 R_p (about 2.7 pooled sigma)" used the per-seed sd; with n = 5 seeds the standard error of the difference is sqrt((0.049² + 0.164²)/5) = 0.077, so the T8 opening in E_ext is **6.1 sigma**. The rms radius (the stable statistic; E_ext is a single-module maximum, sd up to 0.38) opens by +0.122 (SE 0.0089, **13.6 sigma**) for T8 and +0.047 (SE 0.0083, **5.6 sigma**) for T10. The registered E_ext reading of T10 (opening +0.001, SE 0.080) stands as registered, but the rms shows the T10 opening too. G2's opening half is met by rms in both series.
- **Closing, as a trend-relative (r0-type) ratio, rms / [sqrt(3/5)(A/eta)^(1/3)]:** T8 1.0388 (n_t = 0) -> 1.0716 (n_t = 8); slope +0.00409 per triton = **+3.81 mfm per triton** of r0_cp = 0.9300 fm. The corpus slope (NP30 §6) is -4 (median) to -11 (A = 120-140) mfm per triton. **Sign opposite.** Comparison is sign-and-magnitude only (rms over all units against a charge-radius trend).
- **Control, like contact allowed** (post-seen; `nseq19_control_analysis.txt`): T8 ratio 1.0391 -> 1.0666, slope +3.20 mfm per triton; opening in rms +0.103 (SE 0.012, 8.4 sigma). The like-contact exclusion is therefore **not** the cause of the opening expansion or of the positive slope.
- **Size agreement is not evidence of mechanism.** rms ratios of 1.04-1.07 follow from random hard-sphere density: (0.7405 / 0.64)^(1/3) = 1.050, the radius ratio of an eta = pi/sqrt(18) close-pack sphere to a random-close-packed (0.64) sphere. The cube root hides a 15.7 % volume difference. Shared-input (hard spheres), not an independent confirmation of the NP30 packing fraction.
- **Keying fix:** an intermediate version of the control analysis keyed rows by A alone; A = 64 occurs at Z = 28 (T8 end) and Z = 32 (T10 start) and mixed ten samples at n_t = 8. Fixed to key on (Z, A); the numbers above are the fixed ones. `nseq19_series_analysis.py` keyed on (Z, A) throughout.
