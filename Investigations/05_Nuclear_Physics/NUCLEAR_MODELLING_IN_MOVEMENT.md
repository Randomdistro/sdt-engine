## 0. The primitives (strict)

- **MOVEMENT** — the one substance. Matter carries it; space transmits it. Nothing else
  is exchanged, ever.
- **WORK** — movement acting through a span. Two projections only, of the *same* movement:
  - **LINEAR** — movement along a line (what "force × distance" measures).
  - **CIRCULATION** — movement around a closed loop (what "angular momentum / magnetic
    moment" measures).
  Along-axis and around-axis are two shadows of one movement, never two substances.
- **"ENERGY" — not a substance, not a capacity, not a stuff held. Movement only.** Where
  the literature writes *E*, it is **counting an amount of movement** — nothing more. Not
  "the capacity to do work" (a capacity is a held potential — substance-thinking again);
  simply *how much movement there is*. `E = ½mv²` counts the linear movement; `E = mc²`
  counts the movement locked in the rest-tour; `hν` counts the movement in one arrival.
  "A body has energy E" means **there is movement E** — never a filling, a store, or a
  reservoir. Movement is either moving (free) or looping (locked in a tour); it is never
  *stored as* energy, because energy is not a thing that can be stored — it is the tally.

## 1. Rest mass — movement halted by its own opposing movement

`m` is resistance to change of vector (FLM12) — and **resistance to change IS potential,
and potential IS movement halted by an opposing movement.** A proton's rest mass is its
internal tour **self-opposed**: a closed loop is movement that returns on itself, every
part fully moving, the net displacement held still by the opposite part of the same loop.
`m` counts how much movement is currently halted this way. `mc²` is not "mass converted to
energy" — nothing converts substance. The self-opposition is undone and **the same movement
that was halted now runs free**. Halted and free are one movement in two states; the tour
never stopped moving — it stopped *returning on itself*.

## 2. Mass defect — two full movements meshed, sharing opposition (NOTHING stops)

**The mass defect is still two objects moving.** You do not look at two synchronised gears
and say one has stopped — both turn, fully, always. Apart, each nucleon holds its own
balance by **self**-opposition (its rest mass, §1). Meshed — counter-rotating, p +, n −,
the only way gears engage — they oppose **each other**: the opposition is now **shared**, so
less self-opposition is needed to hold the joint balance. The movement formerly halted in
the two separate self-opposed tours is no longer needed to halt, and **runs free** (fragment
recoil, γ). Movement is conserved exactly — **less halted (the defect) = more free (the
release)** — and `E = mc²` is that conservation, not a substance conversion. Both gears keep
turning the whole time. The defect is a **net** reduction: two undiminished movements,
meshed, present less joint resistance than two independent ones, because one shared
opposition halts for both at once.

**The standard defect equates SIZE to energy** (smaller mass = the "missing mass" is
energy); the shadow-**area** reading `κ·ΔA` repeats it — an area × a fitted coefficient
`κ` (MeV/fm²) to make a size read as work. **The coefficient is the confession.** SDT reads
it off the **radius** directly, no coefficient: each object's radius is its tour run at c;
meshing **shares radius** (the interpenetration depth); the shared radius *is* the drop in
independent halting = the defect. NP17 already does it right — deuteron `BE = 3αℏc/D`, `D`
a **radius**-sum, `αℏc` over a length, no κ. The area form (R²=0.988) is the size→energy
approximation; **radius is native** and supersedes it.

## 3. Binding — the work the shared circulation does (NOT a filling in the bond)

NP17: a neutron is `p + internal electron`; that electron is **shared** between adjacent
protons and its **circulation** draws the two positive cores together. "Binding energy" is
**the work that shared-electron circulation performs**, per its tour, holding the cores.
Deuteron: `BE = 3αℏc/D = 2.200 MeV` (meas 2.224) — this number is *the work one shared
tour does across the gap D*, with `αℏc` the only scale (no fitted constant). There is no
"energy in the bond"; there is a circulation doing work.

## 4. THE CENTRAL PICTURE — meshed gears: one synchronisation, measured two ways

Two synchronised counter-rotating gears. Both spin fully; **nothing stops.** Read the one
meshing two ways:

- **as resistance / halting (along)** → the shared opposition halts for both at once → less
  joint resistance than two free gears → **mass defect** ("binding energy").
- **as circulation around the axis** → the two counter-senses partly cancel in the **net**
  while each gear spins full → reduced net loop-movement → **magnetic-moment quench**.

Same meshing. In BOTH the reduction is a **net cancellation of two undiminished movements**,
never a removal of movement — because you don't watch synchronised gears and think one has
stopped. This is why the mass defect and the moment live in the identical interleave and the
identical radius overlap: they are the one synchronisation, read as resistance (along) and
as circulation (around). **The quench is the mass defect's around-face; the same shared
radius gives both.**

**Consequence (the buildable prediction, stated correctly):** the moment quench is not a
separate thing to model or a factor to fit — it is the **around-projection of the same
overlap** already computed to R²=0.988. Take the shared radius's circulation-component where
we have been taking only its resistance-component. Owed: compute the around-projection from
the overlap in hand — same gears, no new parameter.

## 5. The 23.85 MeV of 2d→α — movement freed when two tours mesh into one
