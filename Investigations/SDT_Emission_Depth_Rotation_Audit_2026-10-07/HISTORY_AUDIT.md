## 2. Einstein 1905: kinematic sufficiency does not prove substrate impartation

Einstein's introductory argument removes a privileged stationary-space construction from the electrodynamic formulation. Section 10 derives kinetic energy

\[
K=mc^2(\gamma-1),\qquad \gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}.
\]

The energy divergence at the light-speed limit follows without a mechanical carrier model. The transcript therefore reverses the logical implication: energy divergence is compatible with substrate-derived inertia, but energy divergence cannot establish substrate-derived inertia. Einstein also cautions that historical longitudinal/transverse mass values depend on force and acceleration definitions. Modern invariant rest mass should be separated from velocity-dependent energy and directional inertial response.

Primary source: [Einstein, On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies, introduction and §10, 1905; Perrett–Jeffery translation](https://fourmilab.ch/etexts/einstein/specrel/www/).

## 3. Abraham 1902 and Lorentz 1904: real electromagnetic precedents, bounded conclusions

Abraham's 1902 lecture models an electron as a rigid charged sphere and argues for electromagnetic electron inertia. Extension to the electromagnetic foundation of mechanics appears as a research programme, rather than a completed derivation of every material mass. The closing discussion explicitly assumes continuous aether occupation, extending Maxwell–Hertz equations below the electron radius. A discrete Planck-scale lattice therefore adds a structural hypothesis absent from Abraham's model.

Primary source: [Abraham, Principles of the Dynamics of the Electron, 1902, introduction and conclusion](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation:Principles_of_the_Dynamics_of_the_Electron_(1902)); [German source text and scan linkage](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Prinzipien_der_Dynamik_des_Elektrons_(1902)).

Lorentz's 1904 paper posits deformation of moving electrons and corresponding changes in molecular forces. Under the stated assumptions, longitudinal and transverse inertial coefficients reduce to \(\gamma^3m_0\) and \(\gamma m_0\). Lorentz contrasts the resulting predictions with Abraham's rigid-sphere predictions and confronts contemporary experimental constraints. The paper does not derive SDT baryonic volume, phase-occlusion gravity or a unique pressure constitutive law.

Primary source: [Lorentz, Electromagnetic phenomena in a system moving with any velocity smaller than that of light, 1904, §§8–13](https://pages.jh.edu/rrynasi1/PhysicalPrinciples/literature/Lorentz1904ElectromagneticPhenomenaInASystemMovingWithAnyVelocitySmallerThanThatOfLight.pdf).

## 4. Poincaré: wave replacement and phase lag already received scrutiny

Poincaré's 1905/1906 electron paper develops Lorentz-group structure, additional internal pressure and candidate relativistic gravitational laws. No general theorem in the inspected paper establishes zero force on matter moving through an isotropic radiation bath merely from a baryon delay proportional to \(\gamma\). Lorentz covariance of field equations cannot substitute for transformation of boundary conditions and explicit matter–radiation coupling.

Primary source: [Poincaré, On the Dynamics of the Electron, manuscript dated July 1905, published 1906; scholarly translation](https://henripoincarepapers.univ-nantes.fr/chp/hp-pdf/hp1906rpen.pdf).

In *Science and Method*, Book III, chapter III, §III, Poincaré examines corpuscular shielding, luminous-wave replacement, and Lorentz's 1900 electron phase-lag mechanism. Within the examined model, attraction scales with absorbed energy. Wave language and phase lag therefore do not automatically remove heating. Classical estimates quoted in the text include \(24\times10^{17}c\) for the corpuscular speed lower bound and \(10^{26}\) degrees per second for associated terrestrial heating; the later Lorentz-wave example gives \(10^{12}\) degrees per second. The numbers are model-specific historical estimates, not universal bounds on every imaginable field theory.

Primary source: [Poincaré, The Foundations of Science, authorized 1913 translation, printed pp.517–522](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/39713/pg39713-images.html).

Mechanical solve required for SDT: a conservative scattering/transmission operator, transferred four-momentum, stored field energy, boundary driving, and the force on moving matter. Such a calculation can distinguish an alternative coherent-field mechanism from the historical absorptive mechanism.

## 5. Einstein 1911: a decisive local-clock distinction

The primary 1911 paper derives gravitational clock comparison and the first-order expression

\[
c_K(\Phi)=c_0\left(1+\frac{\Phi}{c_0^2}\right)+O(\Phi^2/c_0^4).
\]

On printed p.906, Einstein explicitly states that identical local clocks measure the same light speed at different positions, before introducing a position-dependent speed using a common time convention. On p.904, the local frequency of an elementary emitter, measured against a colocated clock, remains independent of emitter location. The transcript omits the local-clock distinction and upgrades a global-time result into confirmation of intrinsically pressure-dependent local transmission speed.

The 1911 calculation predicts approximately 0.83 arcseconds of grazing solar deflection. The paper supplies no CMB influx, Planck lattice, density law or source-pressure redshift mechanism.

Primary source: [Einstein, Über den Einfluß der Schwerkraft auf die Ausbreitung des Lichtes, 1911, pp.904–908; original German scan](https://alpha.physics.uoi.gr/foudas_public/ModernPhys-I-UoI-2010/Uber-den-EInfluss-der-Schwerkaft-auf-die-Ausbreitung-des-Lichtes.pdf); [original journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19113401005).

Operational solve required for a physical SDT difference: predict a dimensionless local observable using colocated clock and length standards. A coordinate refractive-index representation alone reproduces a description of gravitational optics without establishing an additional microscopic mechanism.

## 6. Einstein 1920: Leiden, and a constrained ether concept

The address occurred at Leiden on 5 May 1920, rather than Leipzig. Einstein assigns physical qualities to space through gravitational potentials and explicitly disallows trackable mechanical ether parts and an ether state of motion. A lattice of identifiable moving spations is therefore not endorsed by the address. Compatibility would require an effective description respecting the stated restrictions, or an experimentally supported departure.

Einstein also leaves the ether's role in electrical elementary-particle structure unresolved and distinguishes gravitational ether from electromagnetic fields. The transcript's claim of complete absorption of every substrate function into a demonstrated unified mechanism exceeds the address.

Primary source: [Einstein, Ether and the Theory of Relativity, 1920; published English translation](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Einstein_ether/); [original publisher bibliographic record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-50746-5).

## 8. Weyl: gauge redundancy does not establish physical spectral rescaling

Weyl's 1918 programme extends geometric length comparison through an additional connection and attempts unification of gravitation and electromagnetism. Einstein's published addendum objects that history-dependent clock standards would threaten reproducible atomic spectral frequencies; Weyl answers by distinguishing formal vector transport from physical clock dynamics. The exchange explicitly requires a derived clock theory. A local metric rescaling, absent atomic dynamics and measurement rules, cannot establish a measurable SDT emission-depth relation.

Primary source: [Weyl, Gravitation and Electricity, 1918; translation including Einstein's addendum and Weyl's response, PDF pp.12–14](https://www.neo-classical-physics.info/uploads/3/4/3/6/34363841/weyl_-_grav._and_electr.pdf).

The transcript attributes a static-origin redshift account to Weyl 1923. The publisher's primary-work republication identifies *Zur allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie*, Physikalische Zeitschrift 24, 230–232 (1923), as constructing an expanding-universe model. Full 1923 text remained subscription-restricted during the audit, so exact phrase attribution remains unverified. The available record cannot support portrayal of Weyl 1923 as an established advocate of a universally static emission-redshift cosmology.

Primary-work record: [Weyl, On the General Relativity Theory, republication and original publication details](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-009-0826-6).

Independent algebraic check of the transcript's static de Sitter example: with \(f(r)=1-r^2/R^2\), a static emitter at radius \(r\) and static receiver at the origin give

\[
1+z=f(r)^{-1/2},\qquad z=\frac{r^2}{2R^2}+O(r^4/R^4).
\]

The leading dependence is quadratic, rather than the observed low-redshift linear distance relation. Static-coordinate gravitational redshift therefore does not supply the claimed linear law without additional source motion or further structure.

## 9. Hubble: observational caution survives; imagined vindication does not

The 1929 paper establishes an approximate distance–spectral-shift relation over a limited sample. Distances use several stellar and luminosity criteria, rather than Cepheids alone. Hubble explicitly considers de Sitter effects involving apparent atomic slowing and material scattering. The paper provides a historical precedent for separating measured shifts from interpretative dynamics.

Primary source: [Hubble, A Relation Between Distance and Radial Velocity Among Extra-Galactic Nebulae, 1929, pp.168–173](https://www.astro.rug.nl/~weygaert/tim1publication/cosmo2007/literature/hubble.1929.pdf); [original journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.15.3.168).

The Hubble–Tolman joint paper appeared in 1935, not 1937. The abstract and introduction explicitly retain uncertainty between recessional and alternative causes. Galaxy counts required tentative assumptions in both treatments; no decisive confirmation of a static mechanism resulted. The alternative model assumed photon energy loss during transit without transverse scattering and without reduced arrival rate, rather than the SDT source-pressure mechanism.

Primary source: [Hubble and Tolman, Two Methods of Investigating the Nature of the Nebular Red-Shift, 1935, pp.302–337, especially pp.302–304](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1935ApJ....82..302H).

Hubble's 1937 lectures retain cosmological caution but accept ordinary laboratory and stellar Doppler shifts. The lectures explicitly label \(v/c=\Delta\lambda/\lambda\) a small-shift approximation and require observational evidence before replacement of known principles. The non-recession alternative primarily concerns energy loss in intervening space. The transcript's conversion of cosmological caution into Hubble endorsement of source-pressure masking of disk rotation therefore lacks documentary support.

Primary source: [Hubble, The Observational Approach to Cosmology, 1937, chapter II; PDF pp.21–25](https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/Sept04/Hubble/paper.pdf).

Mechanism required for reconsidering cosmological redshift: a quantitative source law, distance relation, arrival-time law, luminosity/area relation and source-population dependence. Mechanism required for reconsidering galactic rotation: a predictive transformation of the approaching/receding differential spectrum, including pressure symmetry and independent velocity constraints. Hubble's caution motivates such tests; Hubble's caution supplies no result for the tests.
