| D3 | gating | The ODF observable for SRA ranging is a word pair of signed 32 bits plus a fractional word; the round-trip light time at 7.37 AU is about 1.0×10⁴ s = 9.4×10¹² RU, which does not fit in 32 bits. ## 2. Stage-1 registration (census; fixed before the run)
**Files.** The four XMMMV0 ODFs of days 157–160 (`cassini_sce1/cors_0021/sce1_DDD/odf/c32easc2002_DDD_0000xmmmv0.odf`), data rows from the label's table row count, 36-byte records with the layout of the GOM37 decoder (time = be32 + 10-bit ms, observable = int32 + frac/10⁹, data type ID, receiver, transmitter, downlink/uplink/exciter band IDs, validity, reference frequency Items 18–19, Items 15, 20–22 the range component fields).
**Census outputs.** Per day: rows by data type; type-37 rows by (receiving station, transmitting station), validity, downlink/uplink/exciter band, time span, count; for the type-37 rows used: finiteness of the decoded observable (C2), min/median/max of the observable, and the Item 15, 20, 21, 22 fields (components and delays) with their distinct values. The rows with validity 0 form the used set.
**Decisions (Stage 1).** C1 holds if the used set has at least one row; if none, PENDING_DATA. C2 holds if every used row decodes finite. The ambiguity M is accepted only if it follows from the component fields (registered form: the observed observable range lies in [0, M) with M a power-of-two multiple of a stated unit) and is reported otherwise as undetermined, in which case Stage 2 compares only differences within a run of constant ambiguity.
**Instrument validation.** The decoder reproduces the GOM37 type-12 row counts for the K252 files (checked here on the XMMMV0 files' type-12 rows against the label ROWS) before the type-37 rows are interpreted.
**Expected landing (hand estimate before registering).** On the order of 800 type-37 rows over the four days; stations 25 (transmit) and 25/45/54 (receive); uplink band X (2), downlink X (2) or Ka (3); the observable below 2³¹ RU (the ambiguity-reduced form).

## 4. Stage-2 registration (fixed before `gom38_reset_2.cpp` exists)
**Reduction.** Rows: type 37, validity 0, receive 25, transmit 25 (432 rows), time tag UTC. Observable in seconds T_obs = obs · RU_sec with RU_sec = (221/1498)·10⁻¹⁸·f_ref (Block-V exciter X-band uplink, f_ref from Items 18–19 of the row: (I18·2²⁴ + I19)/1000 Hz); M_sec = 2²⁵ · RU_sec.
**Model.** Barycentric positions: Earth and Cassini heliocentric (Horizons ecliptic J2000, 15-min, cubic Lagrange interpolation) plus the Sun's barycentric vector (Horizons, ICRF-aligned ecliptic; new raw file `jpl_horizons_sun_barycentric_2002_157_160.csv`, MD5 e44d027ea13fee0b26042865538289d3, DE441). The DSS-25 position from its geodetic coordinates (E-lon 243.1246368°, lat 35.337612°, alt 0.959628 km), WGS-84, rotated by GMST (UT1 ≈ UTC) and IAU-1976 precession into the J2000 equator and then to the ecliptic; nutation and polar motion neglected (declared model error about 0.5 km, 1.5 µs, repeating each sidereal day). Times in TDB = UTC + 64.184 s. Round-trip light time by iteration in the barycentric frame: receive at t_r, turnaround at t_s, transmit at t_u. No relativistic term enters T_model.
**Validations (before the data).** V1 (known answer): the model down-leg, as a light time, against the Horizons DSS-25 range column (delta, converted at 149 597 870.7 km/AU) at all 397 table epochs: gate rms < 3 µs and max < 10 µs. V2: the wrap arithmetic recovers a synthetic offset of 123.456 µs added to the model and wrapped to ±M/2 to 10⁻¹² s.
**Readouts.** (i) Residual ρ = wrap(T_obs − T_model) into (−M_sec/2, M_sec/2]; circular concentration R̄ = |⟨exp(2πi ρ/M_sec)⟩| and the circular standard deviation σ_c = (M_sec/2π)·√(−2 ln R̄) per day and per segment of constant reference frequency; (ii) the Shapiro two-way delay for the frozen K = 1476.35 m: Δ_K = Σ over the up- and down-legs of (2K/c)·ln((r₁ + r₂ + L)/(r₁ + r₂ − L)) (exact chord), its median per day and the ratio to the round-trip light time (C5); (iii) C6: Δ_K/σ_proxy with σ_proxy = σ_c.
**Decisions.** Closure of the Range-Unit conversion: R̄ ≥ 0.9 on a segment. Where R̄ < 0.9 there is no RU closure on that segment and the segment is excluded from any delay comparison (not a K result). If no segment closes: C6 → `RANGE_K_UNRESOLVED` (not exclusion). If segments close, the change-based sensitivity of D4 is registered as a separate pre-registration with the closed segments before it is computed. C7: the Doppler–solar-K path was already DEFINED (grammar) by GOM39; this record does not set PENDING_DEFINITION, because the definition question was closed by GOM39. C8: K frozen, no γ.
**Expected landing (hand estimate before registering).** V1 within 1–2 µs. The row reference frequency spans 7.19–7.21 GHz (Item 18: 428 581 to 429 500, about 2×10⁻⁵ relative spread within a day), and a relative error ε in RU_sec accumulates ε × 8334 s over the round trip; ε of 4×10⁻⁶ already equals M_sec. Closure therefore requires the frequency at the transmit epoch t_u; with the row's f_ref only segments of constant frequency can close. Expected: most of the 432 rows do not close (R̄ < 0.9), a few segments may; Δ_K one-way chord ~6.4×10⁻⁵ s at the SCE1 geometry, two-way ~1.3×10⁻⁴ s, ratio to the round trip ~1.5×10⁻⁸.

## 8. Amendment 2 (diagnostic; registered before `gom38_reset_4.cpp` exists)
Purpose: to characterise the mismatch in each segment of constant reference frequency, not to correct it. For each of the 9 segments (Block-V conversion, the registered one): unwrap the residual sequentially (valid because consecutive steps, a few ms, are far below M ≈ 35.7 ms: gate max |step| < M/4 on every segment, otherwise the segment is reported as not unwrappable), then least-squares fit ρ = a + s·(T_model − T_model,0) and report s, the rms of the fit residual and the span of ρ. Reported only. **No scale is adopted, no segment is declared closed, and K is not touched** (the prompt forbids fitting an RU scale to match Horizons; a slope found here is a diagnostic about the cause of the mismatch, with candidates that are OPEN).
Decision form: if the slopes are the same across segments and the fit rms is far below M, the mismatch is a common smooth rate factor and the cause is to be sought in the definitions (OPEN item, recorded); if the slopes differ between segments, it is segment-dependent. In neither case does the grade of the K question change.
**Expected landing (registered).** A common slope near +0.125 with a fit rms of order 10–100 µs; if the slope differs by segment the cause is not a constant factor.

## 9. Run 4 results (Amendment 2, diagnostic; `gom38_reset_4.cpp`, `gom38_reset_4_results.txt`; single pass)
Within every segment of constant reference frequency the unwrapped residual is **linear in the model round-trip time**: slope s = +0.125302 to +0.125319 in 8 of the 9 segments (day 157: +0.125309, +0.125305; 158: +0.125302, +0.125302, +0.125306; 159: +0.125319, +0.125661 (6 rows); 160: +0.125300), fit rms 3 ns to 40 ns on the short segments (n = 10 to 46: 4.0×10⁻⁸, 1.3×10⁻⁸, 2.5×10⁻⁸, 3.1×10⁻⁹ s) and 5–7 µs on the longer ones (n = 51, 73, 117, spans 1.2–2.5 s of model round-trip change). One segment (day 159, reference frequency pair 429500 / 1280000, 61 rows) is not unwrappable (largest step 13.2 ms against the M/4 = 8.9 ms gate) and is reported only. The observable therefore grows at **1.1253 times** the model round-trip rate on every unwrappable segment, independently of the reference-frequency pair (which varies by 4×10⁻⁵ between segments): a common constant factor, not scatter and not a ramp effect.
Landing against the registration: the slope near +0.125 held (+0.1253); the fit rms was far better than the 10–100 µs registered (3 ns to 7 µs).
Cause: **OPEN.** The factor is not any of the three documented RU definitions (their mutual differences are 0.6% and a factor 3.4). Neighbouring simple ratios do not match to the measured precision of 2×10⁻⁵ (9/8 = 1.125000 is 2.7×10⁻⁴ below; 1498/1331 = 1.12547 is 1.5×10⁻⁴ above). No scale is adopted; the unwrapped residual is not used as a K measurement.
