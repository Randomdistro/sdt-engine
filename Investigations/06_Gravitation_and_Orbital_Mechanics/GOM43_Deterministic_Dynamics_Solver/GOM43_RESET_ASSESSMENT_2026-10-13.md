## 3. Registration addendum (written after reading the example JSONs and the solver manifest, before `gom43_gates_reset_1.py` exists)
- The example `circular-pair` runs 100 steps of 1 s (about 1×10⁻⁵ of its orbital period 1.21×10⁷ s): it is **not** the N1 fixture. N1 is therefore run on a case generated by the script from the example's bodies (the same koppa values 0.001 and 0.002 m, separation 10⁹ m, circular speeds from v² r = c² ϟ_tot), with Δt = P/2000 and duration 100 P (200 000 steps), recording every 2000 steps. The koppa pairwise acceleration is a_i = c² ϟ_j r̂/r²; the independent energy is E = Σ ½ ϟ_i v_i² − c² ϟ₁ϟ₂/r (ϟ playing the role of mass in the per-ϟ bookkeeping).
- The example `simple-pendulum` is a uniform rod (length 1 m, centre of mass at 0.5 m from the pivot, inertia 1/12): a **physical** pendulum with T = 2π√(2L/(3g)) = 1.638 s, not the simple-pendulum T₀ = 2π√(L/g) = 2.006 s of gate R1. R1 is read as a point-mass bob at 1 m from the pivot (link of length 2 m with the pivot anchor at one end and the centre 1 m from it, inertia 10⁻⁶ about the centre), θ₀ = 0.05 rad, g = 9.80665, Δt = 5×10⁻⁴ s, 16 000 steps (8 s, about four periods). The uniform-rod case is also run, as the prompt-ambiguity control: its period is expected at 1.638 s and the R1 relation 2×10⁻³ against 2.006 s is expected to fail by 18%, showing that the gate reads the point-bob fixture. Angle from the link centre: θ = atan2(x_c, −y_c) (pivot at the origin; for the point bob the centre is the bob). Period from upward zero crossings of θ (linear interpolation), mean spacing of consecutive crossings.
- Expected: N1 separation drift and energy drift pass at the registered thresholds (the Verlet drift at P/2000 is of order (2π/2000)² ≈ 10⁻⁵ in phase and the energy oscillation of order 10⁻⁵ relative: the gate 5×10⁻⁶ on energy is **close**; hand estimate: not certain); R1 point-bob period within 2×10⁻³ of 2.00607 s (the amplitude correction is 1.6×10⁻⁴).

**K3 met.** The C-ABI smoke executable exits 0 (no stdout).
**N1 met by wide margin.** Generated case (koppa 0.001 and 0.002 m, separation 10⁹ m, circular speeds from v² r = c²ϟ_tot), P = 1.210037×10⁷ s, Δt = P/2000, 200 000 steps (100 periods), 101 records: max relative separation drift **3.16×10⁻¹³** (gate 5×10⁻⁵); independent relative energy drift at the records **6.3×10⁻¹⁴** (gate 5×10⁻⁶); the solver's own diagnostic over all steps 2.4×10⁻¹¹.
