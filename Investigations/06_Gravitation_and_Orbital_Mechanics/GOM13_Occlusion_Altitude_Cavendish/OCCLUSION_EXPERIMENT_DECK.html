THE FORK · no free parameter on either side02
mechanism

Gravity is two masses shadowing the sky-flux from each other.

The lattice streams convergence flux inward from every direction. A mass occludes it — casts a shadow.

Two masses each sit in the other's shadow, so each is pushed toward the other by the unblocked flux behind it.

No pull. Blocked push. The strength scales with how much flux is actually arriving.

OCCLUSION · Law III03
the key geometry

The Earth blocks the lower sky. You stand in half-shadow.

At the surface the planet fills the lower hemisphere — half your incoming flux is gone.

Climb, and the horizon dips by an angle

δ(h) = arccos( R / (R+h) )

More clear sky returns. The flux recovers. Gravity's driver grows with altitude.

FORCE-vs-ALTITUDE · the headline number05
the instrument

A Cavendish on a disk — four arms, one symmetry.

Four lead bowls and four ball-sets on a perspex disk. 4× the pull, and a symmetry that does the noise rejection in metal, not software:

a shake hauls one arm up, pushes its partner down, the pair cancels — common-mode killed by geometry.

The Earth-shadow is asymmetric — it only darkens the one arm beneath the limb. So it survives the cancellation.

4-ARM DISK · linear acceleration self-cancels06
the trick

GR keeps to the radial line. SDT lifts off — its sightlines slide along the limb.

Red — GR. Only the radial line, shared with the planet's centre, matters. Lifting off changes nothing.

Green — SDT. Lift off and the horizon recedes: the two sightlines stay anchored at the disk and their feet slide outward along the limb. The dip δ on the gauge opens with altitude.

GR vs SDT · the sequence is the fingerprint09
the lock

A force ratio and an angle — the same number, to under 1%.

ΔF/F = sin( β )

Measure the force rise (a pure number) and the tilt budget (an angle in radians) two different ways.

SDT predicts they are the same value at every altitude.

No systematic raises a force by 11% and opens an orientation cone by 0.11 rad in lockstep.

THE LOCK · two observables, one curve10
acceleration drag

Beat the shaking with metal, not electronics.

  • 4-arm symmetry — uniform drag cancels: one up, one down, left/right equalise.
  • Gimbal & self-righting bell — pitch lifts the centre of mass, gravity penalises it, the stack stays vertical to ≪1°.
  • Carousel modulation — push the signal to a quiet band; lock-in throws the rest away.
  • Measure on the descent — slow, controlled, signal disappearing cleanly.
  • Hysteresis null — each disk must go dark on the way down at the same altitude it lit on the way up. Up = down, or it's a lie.
NOISE · passive, mechanical, self-validating11
the platform

Don't burst — cascade. Four balloons become sixteen.

A pinch-valve manifold: when the primary reaches its stretch limit, the thinning air prises the next valve open and helium floods the next chamber.

h_float = H · ln( ρ₀ V / m_total )

Each stage multiplies the volume and lifts the float ceiling. Honest terminus ~50 km — deep in the divergence zone, ΔF/F ≈ +12.5% and still climbing.

Each stage is a quiet neutral-buoyancy station — a ladder of floats at the altitudes the disks fire.
