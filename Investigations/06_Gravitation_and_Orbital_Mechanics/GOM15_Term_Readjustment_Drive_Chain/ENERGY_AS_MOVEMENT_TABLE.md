## The one-paragraph key

Read any energy equation as follows: find whose movement is being counted (matter's closed
path, space's relay, or a queue that can't move); find the boundary it is counted at; and
find the path by which it changes mode. **Energy is conserved because movement has no sink**
(the throughpole never terminates, the cell is always at capacity, and there is only one
speed). Energy "converts" when movement changes mode: PASS→BECOME is absorption and matter
formation; BECOME→PASS is emission and release; anything→PRESS is loading a queue;
PRESS→PASS is the queue finding a door — every exotherm, every chirp, every spring. The
c² in every conversion is not mysterious: it is the exchange rate squared because shares
add in quadrature, and there is only one speed to exchange at.
