## 4. Amendment 1 (registered before `gom_dat9_reset_2.cpp` exists): the ramp census for days 158–160 and the offset of GOM37
**Why.** GOM37 Addendum A found the offset C0 of each constant-reference segment not monotone in the reference frequency Items 18–19 (days 158 and 159 have 3 and 5 reference values). Run 1 shows that the transmitter is constant at 34.3178523 GHz on day 157 and that the (14/15)·f_T relation reproduces the change of the observable to 1.3×10⁻⁴. The transmit tuning is the quantity that enters Fb through the term (T1/T2)(T3·Ft + T4); if it changes between or within days (a ramp, a retune), the constant offset of GOM37 should be stable once the predictor uses the instantaneous f_T instead of a fixed 32.028 GHz.
**Hypothesis H_T (fixed now).** With the predictor P1_c(t) = −2(ṙ_2w/c)·(14/15)·f_T(t − RTLT) (f_T from the ramp record covering the transmit epoch, with its rate), the quantity C0_i = obs_i + P1_c,i is the same constant on every day and in every segment of constant reference frequency. Criterion: for each segment with n ≥ 100 records, |mean(C0_i) − C0_157| < 3 Hz and the within-segment sd < 5 Hz, where C0_157 is the day-157 mean. Segments with a ramp inside (rate ≠ 0) are reported and judged by the same criterion on the sub-segments of constant ramp record. H_T is not supported otherwise; the deviations are then reported against the changes of f_ref and f_T of each segment (descriptive).
**Data.** K252 ODFs of days 157–160, all ramp records of group 25 (the number of data rows from the label's ODF4B25 ROWS); transmit epoch t − RTLT with the 15-minute cubic table; times in seconds since 1950 (the ramp and the orbit group use the same scale).
**Validation.** V4: the ramp records of each day are time ordered and non-overlapping, the ramp interval of the record chosen for a transmit epoch contains it, and every record of the four days finds a ramp record (otherwise reported as uncovered). V5: day 157 of this run reproduces run 1 (C0_157 = mean obs + P1_c with the constant ramp, offset −2 510 006.5 ± 0.1 Hz in the regression, mean value reported).
**Expected landing (hand estimate before looking at the ramp records of days 158–160).** On day 160 (one reference frequency in GOM37) the same constant as on day 157 within a few hertz if f_T did not change (C0 differs by 263 026 Hz in GOM37's global offset: that difference must then come from f_T, since f_ref changed by 8640 Hz). H_T is supported in at least the single-reference segments; segments with ramps may deviate. I assign no probability; the criterion is the test.
**Amendment 1b (registered before the run, from checking the criterion against run 1).** Run 1 gives a slope of −0.99987, not −1: the carrier implied by the change of the observable is 1.26×10⁻⁴ below (14/15)·f_T. With a unit-slope predictor the quantity C0_i = obs_i + P1_c,i carries a term 1.26×10⁻⁴·P1_c,i that varies from day to day with ṙ (a change of 0.5 km/s in ṙ moves it by about 14 Hz), which would exceed the 3 Hz criterion for a reason that is not the offset. The predictor of H_T is therefore P1_c′ = s₁₅₇·P1_c with **s₁₅₇ = 0.999874, calibrated once on day 157** (CALIBRATED(1), a declared parameter whose origin, a 1.26×10⁻⁴ deficit of the effective carrier, is unexplained), and C0_i = obs_i + P1_c′,i. Days 158–160 are then out of sample. The criterion, the data and the validations are unchanged.
