## 5. Addendum A: offset linear in the record's reference frequency (registered after run 1, before it is run)
**Observation that motivates it (run 1).** The reference frequency (Items 18–19) is constant on days 157 and 160 and changes within days 158 and 159; the day-160 offset differs from day 157 by +263 026.3 Hz for a reference change of +8640 Hz (a ratio of 30.44 Hz per Hz). The label's observable is a count rate minus a reference term |Fb·K + B| that is linear in the transmitter and receiver frequencies; if the record's reference frequency is that transmitter frequency, the offset should be a linear function of it, with one slope for the whole file series.
**Model M3.** C0(ref) = C0_157 + κ (ref − ref_157), with C0_d the mean of (obs − f_B) over day d and κ = (C0_160 − C0_157)/(ref_160 − ref_157). Days 157 and 160 (single reference value each) fix both constants; **days 158 and 159 (3 and 5 reference values) are the out-of-sample test**, with no parameter fitted to them.
**Pre-registered criteria.** M3 is supported iff on each of days 158 and 159 the M3 residual (obs − f_B − C0(ref)) has |mean| < 3 Hz and sd < 5 Hz (day 160 has sd 2.1 Hz without the model; the day-157 value is 0.57 Hz). Not supported otherwise (the offset then depends on more than the reference frequency: the ramp history, the receiver frequency, or the bias B).
**Second stage (only if M3 is supported).** The daily mean M3 residuals of days 158 and 159 (days 157 and 160 are zero by construction) against the predicted solar Doppler changes (+0.080 and +0.165 Hz relative to day 157): decidable only if each predicted change exceeds 3 times the standard error of the daily mean (within-day sd/√hours); otherwise UNADJUDICATED_BY_SENSITIVITY stands for the multi-day record 157–160. The day-160 predicted change (+0.259 Hz) is absorbed into κ and is not available as a test (the effect on κ is 10⁻⁶ of 30.44).
**Also reported.** For each reference value on days 158 and 159: the number of records, the segment mean and sd of the M3 residual.
**Expected landing (hand estimate; I have not yet looked at days 158–159 under any model).** If the relation is as assumed, the residuals of days 158 and 159 fall to Hz scale and the criteria are met; if the ramp history or the receiver frequency enters, they remain at kHz. I assign no probability; the criterion is the test. In either case the second stage is expected to fail the sensitivity requirement (scale ≳ 0.3 Hz against predicted changes of 0.08–0.17 Hz).
