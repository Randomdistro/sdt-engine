## 2. Registered quantities (fixed before the run)

**Split.** Train: records with time before the midpoint of the selection; test: the rest.
**Procedure A (prompt literal).** f_A = −2(ṙ_rx/c)·f_ref (f_ref = 2 116 648 992 Hz); s = median(obs/f_A) on train; r_A = obs − s·f_A on test.
**Procedure B (amended).** f_B = +(2/c)·F·ṙ_2w with F = 32.028 GHz fixed (ṙ_2w = ½[deldot(t) + deldot(t − RTLT)]); C0 = mean(obs − f_B) on train; r_B = obs − f_B − C0 on test.
**Solar geometry.** E(t), C(t): Earth and Cassini heliocentric positions at the time tag; r_E, r_C; b(t) = distance from the Sun to the line through E and C (closest-approach point inside the segment checked); the elongation (angle at Earth between the Sun and Cassini); Δt(t) = (2K/c)·ln(4 r_E r_C / b²) with K = 1476.35 m; dΔt/dt by the centred difference of positions at t ± 150 s; the predicted Doppler contribution Δf_pred(t) = 2·F·dΔt/dt (the observable's sign: the obs rises with the equivalent range rate c·dΔt/dt).
**Statistics reported.** Test-half residual min, median, max, sd for A and B; b in solar radii and AU; the elongation; Δt in µs; dΔt/dt; Δf_pred mean and its standard deviation over the test half; corr(r, Δf_pred) and corr(r, 1/b) raw and after removing a linear time trend from each series; the regression coefficient κ = cov(r_B, Δf_pred)/var(Δf_pred) (the SDT value is 1); the ratio sd(r_B)/sd(Δf_pred); the measured test-half residual drift per hour against the predicted drift.
**Decisions.** The prompt's rule on the raw |corr(r, dΔt/dt)| of the test half: ≥ 0.5 SOLAR_K_RESIDUAL_SIGNAL; < 0.2 NO_SOLAR_K_RESIDUAL_SIGNAL; between UNADJUDICATED (C4–C6). Amended outcome: if the sensitivity gate (D4) is not met the outcome is UNADJUDICATED_BY_SENSITIVITY, and the correlation is reported as time-confounded.
**Validations.** V1: the DSS-25 geometry reproduces GOM_DAT8 (the two-way residual sd 2.64 Hz when C0 is fitted on the whole span). V2: the helio tables: |E| in 0.98–1.02 AU and |C| in 7.2–7.6 AU at the epoch; the Earth–Cassini distance from the vectors equals the DSS-25 table's `delta` to 10⁻³ (a test of the two geometries against each other, the station offset being ≤ 6400 km of 1.25×10⁹). V3: the analytic check of the delay formula: Δt for b = 100 R_sun, r_E = 1 AU, r_C = 7 AU equals the closed-form value to 10⁻¹².
**Expected landing (hand estimate before registering).** Elongation about 12°, b about 3×10⁷ km (45 solar radii); Δt about 64 µs; dΔt/dt about −1.5×10⁻¹¹; Δf_pred about −1 Hz and nearly constant (a variation over the test half of the order of 10⁻³ Hz); the sensitivity gate fails by about three orders of magnitude; the raw correlation is large and time-confounded; Procedure A fails to leave a meaningful residual (a level ratio of about −0.295 leaves tens of kHz).

## 5. Direction
1. The prompt's correlation gate cannot be used on a window in which both series are monotone in time: the SOLAR_K_RESIDUAL_SIGNAL it returns is a trend artifact. A valid gate needs either the predicted amplitude above the noise or a design in which the predicted signal changes sign or shape.
2. The predicted effect (1 Hz, nearly constant) can only be separated from the receiver bias with several days of data in which the conjunction geometry changes the delay rate by an amount above the noise: from 6 to 21 June 2002 the elongation falls from about 12° to 0, and b(t) changes by a factor of order 20 (an order-1 variation of the Shapiro Doppler per day). The multi-day ODFs (days 158–160 are in the local folder, not decoded here) are the next step, with a per-day offset (the reference frequency item may change) and a stated bias model; PENDING.
3. The residual structure (2.5 Hz/hour; 1.4 Hz sd) is unmodelled: troposphere at the station, solar-wind plasma along the path at 45 R_sun, and the station's frequency system are candidates, none tested here.
4. Procedure A is retired for Doppler comparisons (level ratio); Procedure B is the amended definition.
