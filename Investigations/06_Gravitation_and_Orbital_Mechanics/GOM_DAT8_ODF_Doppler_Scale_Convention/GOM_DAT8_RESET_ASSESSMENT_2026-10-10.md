**Native Phase B, change-based (registered now).** For a coherent two-way link the counted frequency shift is −(2/c)·F·ṙ with F the downlink carrier. Candidates fixed from the prompt and label constants: F ∈ {2 116 648 992 Hz (the ODF reference), 8.4×10⁹ Hz, 32.028×10⁹ Hz (prompt H7), 34.316×10⁹ Hz (= 32.028×10⁹ × 15/14, my mapping of the label's Ka ratio 14/15)}, each with both signs. ## 6. Direction
1. ## 8. ERRATUM 2026-10-11 (interpolation) and corrected numbers
**Cause.** Phase B and the post-seen residual run interpolated the DSS-25 range-rate tables linearly (5-minute table for the receive epoch, 15-minute table for the up-leg). GOM37 (V4) showed that a 15-minute linear interpolation has a worst error of 7.7×10⁻⁶ relative (19.7 Hz in the observable) and the 5-minute linear about 6 Hz, against 0.02 Hz for 4-point cubic interpolation. My validation VB3 gated the interpolation in relative `deldot` (10⁻⁴), a unit in which the observable's tolerance (Hz) is not visible: a defect of my gate. The run is repeated with the cubic form and nothing else changed (`gom_dat8_reset_2b.cpp`, output `gom_dat8_reset_2b_phaseB_results.txt`; VB1–VB3 met, VB3 now 8.65×10⁻⁹).
**Corrected Phase B (native change-based reading).** P_2w, F = 32.028 GHz, sign flipped: **R = 0.9999 in each third and over the whole span** (previously 1.0006, 1.0006, 0.9999, 1.0002); still the only one of the 16 combinations that meets the criterion (native outcome PASS unchanged). **Unchanged.** The frozen C3 outcome (PASS H6 on a threshold artifact), the identification of the observable as the two-way Doppler of the Ka downlink with the sign flipped and a constant offset, and the direction to GOM_DAT9 (the offset is reference-frequency dependent and not a single-slope law: GOM37 Addendum A).
