## 4. E110 stub (blank-slate version; the Experiments folder is not touched)
**Title.** Decomposition of the deflection coefficient by a powered straight line.
**SDT prediction.** The deflection of a body on a straight line past a depth source is 2c²ϟ/(bv²) for a slow body (geometry factor 2) and 4ϟ/b for light (profile factor 2 × geometry factor 2); ratio θ_slow/θ_light = c²/(2v²). One instrument per factor: the slow body measures the geometry factor alone; light measures the product.
**Standard prediction.** Newtonian small-angle flyby deflection 2GM/(bv²) and the general-relativistic light deflection 4GM/(c²b): the same ratio. The split is the same first-order form (PPN gives it): the stub is the operational decomposition, not a discriminator.
**Observable.** The ratio of the transverse velocity change of a slow probe to the angular deflection of a radio ray at the same impact parameter and source, with ϟ eliminated.
**Apparatus class.** Deep-space probe flybys with Doppler tracking (the slow-body deflection is routinely fitted in gravity-assist navigation) and a radio-interferometric or spacecraft-ranging measurement of the light deflection by the same body; the new element is to report the two coefficients as separately measured numbers with their uncertainties rather than as one fitted mass.
**Status.** The slow-body measurement exists as navigation data (not local); the light measurement is Fomalont's VLBA and the Cassini conjunction (local, γ only). PENDING: the slow-body coefficient from a stated tracking solution.
