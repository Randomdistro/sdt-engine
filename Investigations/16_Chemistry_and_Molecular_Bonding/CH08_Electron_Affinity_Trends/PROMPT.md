## Target

The sign and ordering of electron affinities across the periodic table: why halogens accept
riders eagerly, noble configurations refuse, and the N/O anomaly inverts naive ordering.
Measured anchors: standard electron-affinity tables.

## SDT footing

Affinity is the mirror of ionisation (APS10): the price a nearly-complete void structure
pays — or collects — for seating one more rider. A shell one void short of closure offers a
deep seat (halogens); a closed shell offers none (noble refusal); the half-filled anomalies
are void-geometry effects (Hund-as-triton-integrity is the nuclear cousin of this pattern).
Existing corpus: CH03 (valence tally), CH01/CH02 (bonding as shared/transferred seats),
APS01 (terrain).
